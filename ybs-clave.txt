 Find more information
 For more information on Yukon's key indicators read:
 [https://yukon.ca/en/statistics-and-data/yukon-bureau-statistics/find-demographic-statistics-yukon] population reports ;
 [https://yukon.ca/en/statistics-and-data/yukon-bureau-statistics/find-economic-statistics-yukon] gross domestic product (GDP) by industry growth reports ;
 [https://yukon.ca/en/statistics-and-data/yukon-bureau-statistics/find-employment-statistics-yukon] employment rate reports ;
 [https://yukon.ca/en/statistics-and-data/yukon-bureau-statistics/find-housing-statistics-yukon] real estate transaction reports ;
 [https://yukon.ca/en/statistics-and-data/yukon-bureau-statistics/find-economic-statistics-yukon] change in consumer price index (CPI) reports ; and
 [https://yukon.ca/en/statistics-and-data/yukon-bureau-statistics/find-economic-statistics-yukon] retail sales reports .
 Other indicators can be found in the [https://yukon.ca/en/yukon-fact-sheet-december-2025] Yukon Fact Sheet (December 2025) and the latest [https://yukon.ca/en/statistics-and-data/yukon-bureau-statistics/find-yukons-monthly-statistics] Monthly Statistical Review .
