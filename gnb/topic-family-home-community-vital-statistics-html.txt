 Important information about requesting records for Canadian citizenship by descent. 
 Birth certificates and related
 Birth certificate
 The Vital Statistics Branch registers births and provides certificates of births that have occurred in the province.
 Birth certificate information for land titles transactions for law firms
 Authorized users can order birth certificates for their clients and receive rapid confirmation of names to fulfill land title transactions.
 Birth registration
 Every child born in New Brunswick must be registered within 14 days of the birth.
 Bundled Birth Service
 The Bundled Birth Service provides an easy and efficient way for a parent to access government services related to the birth of their child.
 Change of name or sex designation
 Change of name
 To legally change either name on your birth certificate, contact the Vital Statistics Branch. 
 Change of sex designation
 If you are 16 or older, you may apply to have a change of sex designation on your birth certificate. 
 Death certificates and registration
 Death certificates
 The Vital Statistics Branch provides certificates for deaths that have occurred in the province.
 Death registration
 If a person dies in New Brunswick, the death must be registered with Vital Statistics. 
 Information and resources
 Genealogy research
 Civil registration of vital events has been ongoing in New Brunswick since 1888.
 Marriage
 Civil officiants
 A civil officiant may solemnize civil marriage ceremonies throughout the province. 
 Marriage certificates and licences
 The Vital Statistics Branch registers marriages and provides certificates of marriages that have occurred in the province.
