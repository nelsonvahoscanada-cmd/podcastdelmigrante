 Settlement services
 Settlement service providers help newcomers, including immigrants and refugees, get a successful start in New Brunswick. Their services are free of charge but may vary from one organization to another. These agencies serve as a central hub. They are designated to handle initial needs assessment, orientation, language training, community integration and other support services.
\r\n Find a settlement service provider near you.

 New Brunswick Refugee Clinic
 The refugee claim process can be difficult. If you do not have the financial means to hire a lawyer, the New Brunswick Refugee Clinic may be able to help. The clinic provides free legal services in French or English to asylum seekers in the province. It also helps at-risk persons with applications for permanent residence on humanitarian and compassionate grounds.

 Interim Federal Health Program (IFHP)
 The Interim Federal Health Program can provide limited, temporary coverage of health-care benefits to you if you aren’t eligible for the provincial health insurance but are part of the following groups:
\r\n \r\n refugee claimants
\r\n victims of human trafficking
\r\n victims of domestic violence
\r\n immigration detainees held under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act
\r\n \r\n The program generally covers:
\r\n \r\n inpatient and outpatient hospital services
\r\n services from medical doctors, registered nurses and other health-care professionals licensed in Canada, including pre- and -post-natal care
\r\n laboratory, diagnostic and ambulance services
\r\n Note that the program doesn’t cover all health care services and products.
\r\n \r\n Learn more about the Interim Federal Health Program.&nbsp;

 The Interim Federal Health Program can provide limited, temporary coverage of health-care benefits to you if you aren’t eligible for the provincial health insurance but are part of the following groups:
 refugee claimants
 victims of human trafficking
 victims of domestic violence
 immigration detainees held under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act
 The program generally covers:
 inpatient and outpatient hospital services
 services from medical doctors, registered nurses and other health-care professionals licensed in Canada, including pre- and -post-natal care
 laboratory, diagnostic and ambulance services
 Note that the program doesn’t cover all health care services and products.

 Social assistance
 The Department of Social Development could help you meet your basic needs, such as food, rent, utilities and clothing. Their social assistance program could also help with other needs such as child care, transportation, prescription drugs and more.

 211 New Brunswick
 Being in a new country can be disorienting. 211 specialists can help you find the social, community, health and government services that you may need in New Brunswick. Their service is available in more than 150 languages, 24 hours a day.
