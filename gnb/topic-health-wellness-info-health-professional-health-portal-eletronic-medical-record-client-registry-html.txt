 The Client Registry integration allows EMR users to access current patient demographic information for patients registered in the provincial Client Registry directly from their EMR. 
 Client Registry integration with EMRs 
 The data in the Client Registry includes name, date of birth, gender, NB Medicare number, address and phone number.&nbsp;
\n EMRs can connect to the Client Registry to:
\n \n Query - search the Client Registry for matching patients and retrieve the most current set of demographics, saving time during registration and ensuring EMR charts contain accurate patient data
\n Add/Update - contribute patient demogprahic updates to the Client Registry, helping keep records current, support system-wide data accuracy, and ensure other users have access to the most up-to-date patient information
