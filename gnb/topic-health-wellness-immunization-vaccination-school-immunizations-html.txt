 Vaccines offered in grades 7 and 9 protect students.
 Overview
 The School Immunization Program is a partnership between Public Health and Education and Early Childhood Development to offer free, routine vaccines to students in grades 7 and 9.
\n The vaccines available as part of this program include:
\n In grade 7:
\n \n Human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine 
\n Tetanus, diphtheria and pertussis (Tdap) vaccine 
\n \n In grade 9:
\n \n Meningococcal-C-ACYW
