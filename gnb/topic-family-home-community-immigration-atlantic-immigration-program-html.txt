 Overview
 The Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP) helps employers attract and retain skilled foreign workers by supporting their applications for permanent residence with a valid job or job offer.
\n New Brunswick employers must be designated by Immigration New Brunswick to participate in the program. Once designated, employers can:
\n \n hire qualified candidates for jobs they haven’t been able to fill with Canadian citizens or permanent residents
\n apply to Immigration New Brunswick for an endorsement of each job offer and candidate
\n \n If the endorsement is approved, it can be used to support a permanent residence application.
\n Designated employers can also use the program to support foreign workers already employed in their organizations.

 The Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP) helps employers attract and retain skilled foreign workers by supporting their applications for permanent residence with a valid job or job offer.
 New Brunswick employers must be designated by Immigration New Brunswick to participate in the program. Once designated, employers can:
 hire qualified candidates for jobs they haven’t been able to fill with Canadian citizens or permanent residents
 apply to Immigration New Brunswick for an endorsement of each job offer and candidate
 If the endorsement is approved, it can be used to support a permanent residence application.
 Designated employers can also use the program to support foreign workers already employed in their organizations.

 After endorsement
 As an endorsee, you are expected to apply for permanent residence to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) before your endorsement certificate expires.
\r\n Your family members&nbsp; must be included in your permanent residence application, either as accompanying or non-accompanying.&nbsp;
\r\n Once endorsed, you and your family members cannot apply, or withdraw the endorsement certificate to reapply, under any other program or stream in New Brunswick.
\r\n Learn more about how to apply for permanent residence under the program&nbsp; .
\r\n You must contact Immigration New Brunswick to:
\r\n \r\n report changes in your employment
\r\n correct an error on your endorsement certificate
\r\n ask for a work permit support letter
\r\n ask to withdraw from the program
\r\n \r\n Immigration New Brunswick will then advise you of the next steps and issue you the appropriate documents, if needed.

 Apply for a work permit
 As an endorsee, you may apply to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada for a work permit while waiting for a decision on your permanent residence application.
\n \n You’ll need a letter of support from Immigration New Brunswick
\n You’ll also need an offer of employment number from your employer
\n&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The offer of employment number consists of the letter A followed by a 7-digit number (A1234567)
\n \n To get the offer of employment number, the employer must:
\n \n create an account on the Employer Portal&nbsp; or log in if they already have an account
\n submit an offer of employment
\n pay the compliance fee
\n \n The employer is responsible for the compliance fee, which cannot be taken from you in any way.
\n For more information, visit Immigrate through the Atlantic Immigration Program: Apply for a temporary work permit&nbsp; .

 As an endorsee, you may apply to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada for a work permit while waiting for a decision on your permanent residence application.
 You’ll need a letter of support from Immigration New Brunswick
 You’ll also need an offer of employment number from your employer
 - The offer of employment number consists of the letter A followed by a 7-digit number (A1234567)
 To get the offer of employment number, the employer must:
 create an account on the Employer Portal or log in if they already have an account
 submit an offer of employment
 pay the compliance fee
 The employer is responsible for the compliance fee, which cannot be taken from you in any way.
 For more information, visit Immigrate through the Atlantic Immigration Program: Apply for a temporary work permit .
