EHR Clinical Viewer (ECV) integration
 The EHR Clinical Viewer (ECV) integration allows authorized users to access the Electronic Health Record (EHR) directly from their EMR and view a patient's provincial electronic health record.&nbsp;

 The EHR Clinical Viewer (ECV) integration allows authorized users to access the Electronic Health Record (EHR) directly from their EMR and view a patient's provincial electronic health record. 
 Using ECV to Access a Patient's Information in the EHR
 Users of Certified EMRs must request access to the EHR before using the ECV integration.
\n"}}" id="text-4b501191d6" class="cmp-text">
 Users of Certified EMRs must request access to the EHR before using the ECV integration.
 When viewing a patient chart in the EMR, users can access the EHR by clicking on the ECV button. This will launch ECV in a new window with the EHR patient record in context.
\n Note that ECV is only available for patients with a valid NB Medicare number.
