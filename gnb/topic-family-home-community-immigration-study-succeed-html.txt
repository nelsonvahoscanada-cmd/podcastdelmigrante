 On this page
 Overview
 Study and Succeed in New Brunswick is a province-wide initiative. It is designed to help international students studying in New Brunswick’s post-secondary institutions integrate into the local community and workforce, preparing them for long-term success after graduation.
\n"}}" id="text-bde1738a2c" class="cmp-text">
 Study and Succeed in New Brunswick is a province-wide initiative. It is designed to help international students studying in New Brunswick’s post-secondary institutions integrate into the local community and workforce, preparing them for long-term success after graduation.
 Eligibility
 To be eligible, you must be:
\n \n an international student
\n studying at a post-secondary institution in New Brunswick
\n \n The program accepts three cohorts per year:
\n \n January
\n May
\n September
\n \n Your college or university will inform you when applications are being accepted.

 Selected international students will:
 have access to the program in both official languages
 participate in a mandatory three-day, in-person conference, which focuses on:
 - cultural adaptation
 - career and labour market information
 - networking
 - immigration pathways
 ongoing support and communication, including provincial immigration resources, from the International Student Integration team of Immigration New Brunswick
 Participants in the final half of their program of study may be eligible for the mentorship component.
