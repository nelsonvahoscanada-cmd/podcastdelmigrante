 Overview
 \n Data purpose:&nbsp; To show the limits of the rural districts which cover areas outside incorporated local governments for the provision of services such as fire protection, streetlights and garbage disposal.
\n Data description:&nbsp; Rural district (RD) polygons are a graphical representation of the rural district boundaries as defined in regulation 2022-45 under the Local Governance Act with an associated RD name attribute. Effective date is January 1, 2023.
\n Update requirements:&nbsp; On an ad hoc basis as boundaries change.
\n Restriction on use:&nbsp; Unrestricted
\n License:&nbsp; Open Government Licence 
\n Georeferencing:&nbsp; datum - NAD83(CSRS), map projection - NB Stereographic Double, (EPSG 2953)
\n Data formats:&nbsp; ESRI file geodatabase, ESRI shape file, KML
\n Data coverage and size:&nbsp; Provincial coverage
\n Responsible Agency:&nbsp; Department of Environment &amp; Local Government

 Data purpose: To show the limits of the rural districts which cover areas outside incorporated local governments for the provision of services such as fire protection, streetlights and garbage disposal.
 Data description: Rural district (RD) polygons are a graphical representation of the rural district boundaries as defined in regulation 2022-45 under the Local Governance Act with an associated RD name attribute. Effective date is January 1, 2023.
 Update requirements: On an ad hoc basis as boundaries change.
 Restriction on use: Unrestricted
 License: Open Government Licence 
 Georeferencing: datum - NAD83(CSRS), map projection - NB Stereographic Double, (EPSG 2953)
 Data formats: ESRI file geodatabase, ESRI shape file, KML
 Data coverage and size: Provincial coverage
 Responsible Agency: Department of Environment & Local Government
