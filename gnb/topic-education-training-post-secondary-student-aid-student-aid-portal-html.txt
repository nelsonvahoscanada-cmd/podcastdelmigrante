 To complete your student aid account, you need a valid Social Insurance Number (SIN). Accounts created without providing a valid SIN will be deleted.
 Only one account can be created for each SIN. If you have already registered but can’t sign in because you forgot your password or answers to your security questions, call us at 1‑800‑667‑5626 and we will help you access your account.
