 Schools in New Brunswick work with schools abroad to give students the opportunity to study, work or improve language skills abroad. Exchange programs give New Brunswick students the chance to spend a semester or full academic year at a partner institution abroad, while earning credits that count toward their program.
 To take part, you must be a registered student at one of the following public institutions in New Brunswick:
 Mount Allison University Anglophone 
 St. Thomas University Anglophone 
 University of New Brunswick Fredericton, Saint John Anglophone 
 Université de Moncton Francophone 
 New Brunswick Community College Anglophone
