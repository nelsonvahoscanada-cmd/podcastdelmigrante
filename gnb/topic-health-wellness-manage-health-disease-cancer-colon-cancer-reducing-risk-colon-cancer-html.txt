 Lowering your risk
 You can lower your risk for colon cancer by:
\r\n \r\n getting screened regularly
\r\n eating a balanced diet high in fruits and vegetables
\r\n maintaining a healthy weight
\r\n exercising
\r\n not smoking
\r\n limiting alcohol consumption
\r\n taking vitamin D supplements
\r\n \r\n If you have questions or concerns about colon cancer, speak with a primary health-care provider or visit cancer.ca.&nbsp;

 You can lower your risk for colon cancer by:
 getting screened regularly
 eating a balanced diet high in fruits and vegetables
 maintaining a healthy weight
 exercising
 not smoking
 limiting alcohol consumption
 taking vitamin D supplements
 If you have questions or concerns about colon cancer, speak with a primary health-care provider or visit cancer.ca.
