New Brunswick radiation therapy wait times
 The New Brunswick Cancer Network reports on the nationally recognized benchmark for wait times for radiation therapy.
\n"}}" id="text-945220b790" class="cmp-text">
 The New Brunswick Cancer Network reports on the nationally recognized benchmark for wait times for radiation therapy.
 Overview
 On a monthly basis, the New Brunswick Cancer Network reports the percentage of patients receiving their first radiation therapy treatment within four weeks of being ready to treat. This information comes from the two cancer centers in New Brunswick.
