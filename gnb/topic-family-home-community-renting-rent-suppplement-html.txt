 Overview
 Housing NB has agreements with private landlords for several subsidized housing units. This program funds the difference between the rent that a household pays, and the unit's market value rent. The unit is subsidized, otherwise, it is a normal landlord-tenant relationship.&nbsp;
\r\n Subsidized rental accommodations are provided to those whose income is below the established income ceilings. Tenants who qualify through the program will have their rents reduced to 30% of the adjusted household income.
\r\n"}}" id="text-70ed2b544c" class="cmp-text">
 Housing NB has agreements with private landlords for several subsidized housing units. This program funds the difference between the rent that a household pays, and the unit's market value rent. The unit is subsidized, otherwise, it is a normal landlord-tenant relationship. 
 Subsidized rental accommodations are provided to those whose income is below the established income ceilings. Tenants who qualify through the program will have their rents reduced to 30% of the adjusted household income.
