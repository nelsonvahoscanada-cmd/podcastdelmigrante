 Overview
 The purpose of the Clinical Supervision program is to remunerate fee-for-service physicians who supervise nurse practitioner students during clinical placements.
\r\n A maximum of 700 hours per student will be permitted.

 The purpose of the Clinical Supervision program is to remunerate fee-for-service physicians who supervise nurse practitioner students during clinical placements.
 A maximum of 700 hours per student will be permitted.
 Eligibility
 Physicians must: 
\n \n be a fee-for-service physician practicing in New Brunswick
\n have a Service New Brunswick supplier number associated with the NP Clinical Supervision Program for payment (physicians may contact&nbsp; hwp-pess@gnb.ca &nbsp;for assistance in obtaining a supplier number for this program)
\n \n Nurse practitioner students must: 
\n \n be a resident who has lived in New Brunswick for at least one year prior to entering the nurse practitioner program
\n be a nurse registered with the College of Nurses of New Brunswick (CNNB)
\n be enrolled in a full or part‐time nurse practitioner program at a recognized university in Canada
\n complete invoiced clinical placements in New Brunswick
\n \n The university must: 
\n \n keep a record of invoiced student clinical hours and dates
\n submit the request to&nbsp; hwp-pess@gnb.ca
