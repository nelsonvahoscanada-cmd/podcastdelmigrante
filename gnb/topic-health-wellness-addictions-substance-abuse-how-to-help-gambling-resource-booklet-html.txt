 Message to family members, supportive friends, and peers of an individual experiencing gambling-related harms
 Overview
 Supporting or living with someone affected by gambling‑related challenges can bring a range of emotions. It is common to experience stress, uncertainty, or mixed feelings, which can affect your own well‑being. Support is available for family members, friends, and peers, and reaching out to trusted people or professionals can help, you do not have to face this on your own.
