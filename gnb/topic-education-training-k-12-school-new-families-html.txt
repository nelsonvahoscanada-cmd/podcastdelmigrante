 On this page
 Overview
 This video series is designed to support newcomer families in learning about the anglophone education system in New Brunswick. This video series will help you learn about the anglophone education system in our province, enabling you to assist your child or children in adjusting to their new environment and feeling more confident. This video provides important information on how schools are organized, when and where to enroll your children, what support services are available and how lunchtime works, among other topics.
