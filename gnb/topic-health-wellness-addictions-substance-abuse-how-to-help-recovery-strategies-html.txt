 Find your way back from gambling
 Overview
 This brochure provides recovery strategies for individuals experiencing significant concerns related to gambling, but it may also be useful to others. The strategies listed below reflect approaches commonly used by clinicians and individuals experiencing gambling-related harms. They may offer practical support for you or others in similar situations.
