 Recognizing excellence across New Brunswick’s early learning and child care sector
 Overview
 The Minister’s Excellence in Early Learning and Child Care Awards&nbsp;honour&nbsp;early childhood professionals who&nbsp;demonstrate&nbsp;outstanding commitment to high-quality practice, leadership and support for children and families.
\n Areas of excellence include:
\n \n innovative educational practice and leadership
\n responding to the diverse needs of all learners
\n lifelong learning
\n fostering a positive learning environment
\n \n Early childhood professionals currently working in licensed early learning and&nbsp;child care&nbsp;settings across New Brunswick’s Anglophone school districts are eligible for nomination. Nominations can be submitted by parents, colleagues, service&nbsp;providers&nbsp;or community partners.
\n"}}" id="text-188227a724" class="cmp-text">
 The Minister’s Excellence in Early Learning and Child Care Awards honour early childhood professionals who demonstrate outstanding commitment to high-quality practice, leadership and support for children and families.
 Areas of excellence include:
 innovative educational practice and leadership
 responding to the diverse needs of all learners
 lifelong learning
 fostering a positive learning environment
 Early childhood professionals currently working in licensed early learning and child care settings across New Brunswick’s Anglophone school districts are eligible for nomination. Nominations can be submitted by parents, colleagues, service providers or community partners.
