 The New Brunswick Health Portal is a gateway to modern, secure health care solutions that improve patient care and health system efficiency across the province. 
 The Electronic Health Record (EHR) is a tool which provides health professionals with access to their patient’s health information from across the province to improve coordination of care. 
 eConsult (for family physicians and nurse practitioners and for specialists ) and CommunityCareNB promote collaboration and remote care through innovative technologies, while the Provincial Electronic Medical Record (EMR) program provides EMR vendors with an informational framework on how to certify EMRs for use in the community. 
 Together, these services align with the vision of "One Patient, One Record" by prioritizing real-time accessibility, integration, data quality and security in health-care delivery.

 New Brunswick Health Council
 The New Brunswick Health Council has a dual mandate: to report publicly on the performance of the provincial health system, and to engage citizens in the improvement of health service quality. 
 Service New Brunswick
 Service New Brunswick is the Government of New Brunswick’s chief provider of front-line services to the public.
