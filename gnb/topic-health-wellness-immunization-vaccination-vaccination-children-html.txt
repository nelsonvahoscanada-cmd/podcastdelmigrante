 Overview
 Vaccination is one of our most important public health tools. Over the past 50 years, vaccination has saved many lives in Canada and around the world. Some childhood diseases that were once common in Canada are now rare because of vaccines.
\r\n Vaccines will protect your child (and community) against serious illness and disease.&nbsp;It’s&nbsp;important for children to&nbsp;receive&nbsp;all recommended vaccinations on time&nbsp;for&nbsp;the best protection against vaccine preventable diseases.
\r\n When your child enters a New Brunswick school for the first time, you are required under the&nbsp; Education Act &nbsp;and the&nbsp; Public&nbsp;Health Act &nbsp;to provide proof of immunization. Proof of immunization is also&nbsp;required&nbsp;for children who attend a day care center.&nbsp;The law allows parents&nbsp;or guardians&nbsp;to refuse immunization based on medical reasons or for reasons of religious belief or conscience.&nbsp;An exemption form is available from your local Public Health office.

 Vaccination is one of our most important public health tools. Over the past 50 years, vaccination has saved many lives in Canada and around the world. Some childhood diseases that were once common in Canada are now rare because of vaccines.
 Vaccines will protect your child (and community) against serious illness and disease. It’s important for children to receive all recommended vaccinations on time for the best protection against vaccine preventable diseases.
 When your child enters a New Brunswick school for the first time, you are required under the Education Act and the Public Health Act to provide proof of immunization. Proof of immunization is also required for children who attend a day care center. The law allows parents or guardians to refuse immunization based on medical reasons or for reasons of religious belief or conscience. An exemption form is available from your local Public Health office.
 Childhood vaccination schedule
 Your child will need to be vaccinated at&nbsp;different times&nbsp;in their life to get the best protection against vaccine-preventable diseases.&nbsp;Vaccination schedules&nbsp;are designed to provide protection before children are exposed to the disease.&nbsp;
\r\n Childhood vaccines are free&nbsp;of charge&nbsp;and offered according&nbsp;to&nbsp;routine or catch-up schedules.&nbsp;Contact your primary care provider or&nbsp;your local&nbsp;Public Health&nbsp;office&nbsp;to schedule an appointment for your child’s immunization.

 Immunization records
 Vaccination records for people aged 16 and older are available by registering online at MyHealthNB . To access your immunization record, please register or sign&nbsp;in to&nbsp;your account.

 Vaccination records for people aged 16 and older are available by registering online at MyHealthNB . To access your immunization record, please register or sign in to your account.
 The paper record of immunization provided when vaccinated will continue to be the official record. Keep your immunization record in a safe place.
 If the information on your record is incorrect or you received immunizations outside of New Brunswick and want them added, send an email to [email protected] .
 Immunizations administered prior to 1991 may not be readily accessible in MyHealthNB .  If your vaccination record is not available at MyHealthNB , contact the place you received your vaccines.
