 The Prescription Monitoring Program (PMP) aims to reduce prescription drug misuse, support safer and coordinated patient care and promote appropriate prescribing practices. Prescription information from all community pharmacies within New Brunswick is used for the PMP. Authorized health-care providers can access and use this to make more informed decisions about patient care. The privacy and security of patient information is strictly enforced. Health-care providers may access information only for patients under their care.
 The program receives recommendations from the PMP Advisory Committee, composed of members representing multiple health professions, including pharmacy, medicine, nursing, dentistry, optometrists and veterinarians.
 The New Brunswick Prescription Monitoring Program is regulated under the Prescription Monitoring Act and its PMP Regulation .
 Monitored drugs
 Monitored drugs include opioids, such as Dilaudid (hydromorphone) and Percocet (oxycodone), and controlled drugs like Ritalin (methylphenidate) and Ativan (lorazepam).

 New Brunswick has launched a new program called MaveRx NB (pronounced “Mavericks”). As the first prescription monitoring technology solution in Canada to include prescription information on all drugs, MaveRx NB provides access to prescription information and helps health-care providers assess patients.
 For example, prescribers can assess risk of harm due to:
 potentially high doses and long-term use
 multiple prescriptions for similar monitored drugs
 different pharmacies where prescriptions have been filled
 possible risky combinations of monitored drugs
 Health-care providers can also draw insights from their own individualized prescriber reports. This helps them assess how their prescribing may affect patient safety. MaveRx NB also allows the provider’s team to act as delegates to access the system and search for patients on their behalf.
