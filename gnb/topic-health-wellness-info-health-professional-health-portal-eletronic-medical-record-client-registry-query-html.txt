 Best practices to be followed by clinic users when interacting with a patient and querying the Client Registry.
 Overview
 These steps help ensure the most accurate and up-to-date information is retrieved and recorded during the clinical encounter.
\r\n The Client Registry integration enables EMR users to retrieve up-to-date patient demographic information directly from the provincial Client Registry. Available data includes name, date of birth, gender, NB Medicare number, address, and phone number. Through the CR Query, users can search for matching patients and import the most current demographics, reducing registration time and ensuring EMR charts remain accurate and consistent.

 These steps help ensure the most accurate and up-to-date information is retrieved and recorded during the clinical encounter.
 The Client Registry integration enables EMR users to retrieve up-to-date patient demographic information directly from the provincial Client Registry. Available data includes name, date of birth, gender, NB Medicare number, address, and phone number. Through the CR Query, users can search for matching patients and import the most current demographics, reducing registration time and ensuring EMR charts remain accurate and consistent.
