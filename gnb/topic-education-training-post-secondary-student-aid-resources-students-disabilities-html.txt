 Information on additional support for eligible students with disabilities.
 On this page
 Definition of disability
 Permanent disability: &nbsp;any impairment, including a physical, mental, intellectual, cognitive, learning, communication or sensory impairment—or a functional limitation—that restricts the ability of a person to perform the daily activities necessary to pursue studies at a post-secondary school level or to participate in the labour force and that is expected to remain with the person for the person’s expected life.
\r\n Persistent or prolonged disability: &nbsp;any impairment, including a physical, mental, intellectual, cognitive, learning, communication or sensory impairment—or a functional limitation—that restricts the ability of a person to perform the daily activities necessary to pursue studies at a post-secondary school level or to participate in the labour force and has lasted, or is expected to last, for a period of at least 12 months, but is not expected to remain with the person for the person’s expected life.
\r\n Not all medical conditions are considered disabilities for the purpose of Canada Student Financial Assistance Program funding.

 Applying as a student with a disability
 To be considered as a student with a disability, you must: 
\n \n self-identify as having a disability and attest that your disability impacts your studies when completing your application for student financial assistance 
\n submit the Disability Verification Form (PDF 1 MB) &nbsp;which normally only must be submitted once for verification of your eligibility as a student with a disability
\n if you are applying based on learning disability diagnosis, submit Section A of the disability verification form and a copy of your psychoeducational assessment completed by a registered psychologist within five years of the date of your application (or completed when you were 18 years of age or older)
\n submit the Program Information Form (PDF 1.4 MB)

 To be considered as a student with a disability, you must: 
 self-identify as having a disability and attest that your disability impacts your studies when completing your application for student financial assistance 
 submit the Disability Verification Form (PDF 1 MB) which normally only must be submitted once for verification of your eligibility as a student with a disability
 if you are applying based on learning disability diagnosis, submit Section A of the disability verification form and a copy of your psychoeducational assessment completed by a registered psychologist within five years of the date of your application (or completed when you were 18 years of age or older)
 submit the Program Information Form (PDF 1.4 MB) 
 Supports for students with disabilities
 As a student with an approved disability you may:
\r\n \r\n be exempt from making the Fixed Student Contribution*
\r\n be eligible for a non-repayable grant of $2,800 per academic year*
\r\n qualify for full-time assistance with smaller, more manageable course loads (at least 40% of a full course load)*
\r\n have access to extra weeks of funding (520 weeks) to complete your program with the help of student aid
\r\n qualify for extra funding specifically designed to help you pay for exceptional educational-related expenses related to your approved disability*
\r\n \r\n *For students with a persistent or prolonged disability, these supports are available only during years in which you have been approved for persistent or prolonged disability status. 
\r\n"}}" id="text-e105a9f1f3" class="cmp-text">

 Additional funding available for students with disabilities
 There are two grants available for students with disabilities who qualify for federal student aid.
\r\n"}}" id="text-568ff7dd0f" class="cmp-text">
 There are two grants available for students with disabilities who qualify for federal student aid.
 Canada student grant for students with disabilities
 This grant provides $2,800 per academic year to help reduce the costs students with disabilities face when participating in a post-secondary program.
\r\n There is no need to apply for this grant. You will be considered for this grant during the assessment of your application for student financial assistance, if you have provided the required disability information.
\r\n"}}" id="text-fcd65df809" class="cmp-text">
 This grant provides $2,800 per academic year to help reduce the costs students with disabilities face when participating in a post-secondary program.
 There is no need to apply for this grant. You will be considered for this grant during the assessment of your application for student financial assistance, if you have provided the required disability information.
 Canada student grant for services and equipment
 Students with disabilities can receive up to a maximum of $20,000 per academic year to&nbsp;purchase&nbsp;education related services and assistive equipment which are not provided by&nbsp;their&nbsp;educational institution. Examples are tutors, note-takers, interpreters,&nbsp;and technical aids.
\r\n To be considered for this grant, you must:
\r\n \r\n have&nbsp;submitted&nbsp;your&nbsp; application for student financial assistance &nbsp;and have been approved for&nbsp;federal&nbsp;funding as a student with a disability
\r\n submit the application for Canada student grant for services and equipment – students with disabilities and all required supporting documents no later than six weeks before the end date of your period of study
\r\n \r\n"}}" id="text-5155c925fc" class="cmp-text">
 Students with disabilities can receive up to a maximum of $20,000 per academic year to purchase education related services and assistive equipment which are not provided by their educational institution. Examples are tutors, note-takers, interpreters, and technical aids.
 To be considered for this grant, you must:
 have submitted your application for student financial assistance and have been approved for federal funding as a student with a disability
 submit the application for Canada student grant for services and equipment – students with disabilities and all required supporting documents no later than six weeks before the end date of your period of study
 Note: &nbsp; If you did not qualify for Canada student financial assistance when your application was assessed, contact&nbsp; Student Financial Services &nbsp; for additional information regarding your eligibility.
