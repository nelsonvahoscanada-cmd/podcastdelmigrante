 Radiation therapy wait times
 The New Brunswick Cancer Network reports on the nationally recognized benchmark for wait times for radiation therapy. On a monthly basis, NBCN reports the percentage of patients receiving their first radiation therapy treatment within four weeks of being ready to treat. This information comes from the two cancer centers in New Brunswick.
