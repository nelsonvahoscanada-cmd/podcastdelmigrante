 Overview
 CommunityCareNB is a patient health record system designed to support coordinated care in community-based programs. CommunityCareNB is currently used by the Extra-Mural Program for maintaining a patient’s health record and includes patient care plans recent assessments, interventions and care observations. CommunityCareNB is also used by Ambulance New Brunswick to capture a patient electronic Patient Care Record (ePCR). CommunityCareNB is also integrated with the Provincial Electronic Health Record (EHR). The platform is built on Salesforce Health Cloud, ensuring reliable and efficient access to patient information for better collaboration and care.&nbsp;&nbsp;

 CommunityCareNB is a patient health record system designed to support coordinated care in community-based programs. CommunityCareNB is currently used by the Extra-Mural Program for maintaining a patient’s health record and includes patient care plans recent assessments, interventions and care observations. CommunityCareNB is also used by Ambulance New Brunswick to capture a patient electronic Patient Care Record (ePCR). CommunityCareNB is also integrated with the Provincial Electronic Health Record (EHR). The platform is built on Salesforce Health Cloud, ensuring reliable and efficient access to patient information for better collaboration and care. 
 Today nurse practitioners and physicians can use CommunityCareNB to: 
 create a Referral for Extra-Mural Program (EMP) services
 monitor EMP Referrals. Will not need to call EMP to determine the status of a referral
 review a patient's dispensed medication list from all NB-based Community Pharmacies Hospital-based users can print a Medication Reconciliation Report for hospital admissions
 review a patient’s electronic health record including care plans, recent assessments, interventions and care observations 
 link with Provincial Client Registry to search, add or update a person account 
 see a patient's hospital visits
