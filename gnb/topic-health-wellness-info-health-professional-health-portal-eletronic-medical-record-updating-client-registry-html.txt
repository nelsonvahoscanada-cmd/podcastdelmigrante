Updating the Client Registry
 The following guidelines outline best practices for adding or updating a patient in a Certified EMR.
\n"}}" id="text-7f54b17f47" class="cmp-text">
 The following guidelines outline best practices for adding or updating a patient in a Certified EMR.
 Overview
 The Client Registry integration enables EMR users to access current patient demographic information directly from the provincial Client Registry. The CR Add/Update functionality allows EMRs to contribute updated patient demographics back to the Client Registry, helping keep records current, support system-wide data accuracy, and ensure other users have access to the most up-to-date information.
\r\n Field labels may vary by EMR, so the guidelines below focus on general practices to ensure updates are accurate. For EMR-specific instructions, please refer to training provided by your EMR vendor.
\r\n This guide is also available as a PDF download:&nbsp; Adding and Updating Patient Information Quick Reference Card (PDF 4 MB)
