Rural and Indigenous housing program
 A program for households in rural communities and off-reserve Indigenous households to obtain affordable housing.

 Overview
 The program helps low-income households find affordable and suitable rental housing in rural communities.&nbsp; It also helps eligible off-reserve Indigenous households obtain affordable and suitable housing in rural communities with a population of less than 2,500.
\r\n Subsidized rental accommodations are provided to those whose income is below the established income ceilings. Tenants who qualify through the program will have their rents reduced to 30% of the adjusted household income.

 The program helps low-income households find affordable and suitable rental housing in rural communities. It also helps eligible off-reserve Indigenous households obtain affordable and suitable housing in rural communities with a population of less than 2,500.
 Subsidized rental accommodations are provided to those whose income is below the established income ceilings. Tenants who qualify through the program will have their rents reduced to 30% of the adjusted household income.
