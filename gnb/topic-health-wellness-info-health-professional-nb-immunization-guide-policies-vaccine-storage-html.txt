Vaccine storage, handling and transportation of vaccines
 This policy outlines the best practices for the storage, handling, and transportation of publicly funded vaccines in New Brunswick (NB).

 This policy outlines the best practices for the storage, handling, and transportation of publicly funded vaccines in New Brunswick (NB).
 Overview
 To ensure the integrity and efficacy of vaccines throughout their lifecycle, minimizing risks such as compromised immunizing agents and potential transmission of vaccine-preventable diseases, while adhering to regulatory requirements. This policy aims to address vaccine management guidelines for all immunization providers, decreasing vaccine losses, maintaining public confidence, and maximizing public health outcomes.&nbsp;
