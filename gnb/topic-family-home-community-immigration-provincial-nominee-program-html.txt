 Overview
 The New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program (NBPNP) offers pathways to permanent residence for foreign nationals who wish to live and work, or run a business, in New Brunswick.
\n While Immigration New Brunswick selects and nominates candidates based on their skills, education and work experience in relation to New Brunswick’s economic needs, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) makes the final decision on applications for permanent residence.

 The New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program (NBPNP) offers pathways to permanent residence for foreign nationals who wish to live and work, or run a business, in New Brunswick.
 While Immigration New Brunswick selects and nominates candidates based on their skills, education and work experience in relation to New Brunswick’s economic needs, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) makes the final decision on applications for permanent residence.

 Multiple expressions of interest or applications 
 An INB profile can have only one active expression of interest or application. 
\n If you want to submit an endorsement application to the Atlantic Immigration Program candidate pool and submit an expression of interest to the New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program candidate pool, you must create a separate INB profile using a different email address for each program. You can only have one active application with Immigration New Brunswick. This means you can be in both pools, but you can only move forward with one of them.
\n If you receive an invitation to apply for a New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program stream, you must withdraw all your other submissions.
\n If your Atlantic Immigration Program endorsement application is selected for processing, you must withdraw all your expressions of interest.
\n If you receive an invitation to apply for a New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program stream and your Atlantic Immigration Program endorsement application is selected for processing, you must choose only one and withdraw the other.
\n If you submit more than one active application, all of them could be refused.

 Invitations to apply
 Submitted expressions of interest are placed in the New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program candidate pool. They remain valid for 365 days from the date of submission.
\n Immigration New Brunswick reviews submitted expressions of interest and issues invitations to apply only to selected individuals based on provincial labour market needs, available allocation and other priorities determined by the Government of New Brunswick.
\n The following streams are subject to scheduled invitation draws:
\n \n New Brunswick Skilled Worker stream
\n New Brunswick Express Entry stream
\n New Brunswick Strategic Initiative

 Decision 
 If your application is successful, you will receive a nomination certificate through the INB portal. This is the only way a certificate is issued.
\n If you cannot satisfy Immigration New Brunswick that you meet all requirements, your application will be refused. You will receive a refusal letter through the INB portal.

 If your application is successful, you will receive a nomination certificate through the INB portal. This is the only way a certificate is issued.
 If you cannot satisfy Immigration New Brunswick that you meet all requirements, your application will be refused. You will receive a refusal letter through the INB portal.
 After nomination
 As a nominee, you are expected to apply for permanent residence to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) before your endorsement certificate expires.
\n Your family members&nbsp; must be included in your permanent residence application, either as accompanying or non-accompanying.&nbsp;
\n Once nominated, you and your family members cannot apply, or withdraw the nomination certificate to reapply, under any other program or stream in New Brunswick.
\n There are two ways to apply for permanent residence. Your certificate will indicate whether you can apply through:
\n \n the Express Entry process&nbsp; 
\n the non–Express Entry process&nbsp; 
\n \n You must contact Immigration New Brunswick to:
\n \n report changes in employment
\n correct an error on your nomination certificate
\n ask for a work permit support letter
\n ask to extend the validity of your nomination certificate
\n withdraw from the program
\n \n Immigration New Brunswick will then advise you of the next steps and issue you the appropriate documents, if needed.
\n Fill out one the following forms to contact Immigration New Brunswick:
\n \n NB-021 Post-Nomination Requests (PDF 286 KB) 
\n NB-011 Withdrawal of Nomination Application or Certificate (PDF 380 KB)

 Apply for a work permit
 As a provincial nominee, you may apply to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada for a work permit while waiting for a decision on your permanent residence application.
\r\n Bridging open work permit
\r\n You may apply for this on your own if you qualify
 Find out if you are eligible

 \r\n Closed work permit with employer support
\r\n You’ll need a letter of support from Immigration New Brunswick
 You’ll also need an offer of employment number from your employer
\r\n - The offer of employment number consists of the letter A followed by a 7-digit number (A1234567)
 \r\n To obtain the offer of employment number, the employer must: 
\r\n create an account on the Employer Portal or log in if they already have an account
 submit an offer of employment
 pay the compliance fee
 \r\n The employer is responsible for the compliance fee, which cannot be taken from you in any way.
