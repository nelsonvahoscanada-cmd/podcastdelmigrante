 Overview
 The Workforce Development branch at Immigration New Brunswick partners with employers across the province to address workforce needs through targeted recruitment initiatives and employer engagement activities.
\n The branch coordinates a wide range of recruitment opportunities throughout the year, including provincial, national, virtual and international recruitment events. These events help employers connect with qualified talent, from New Brunswick, across Canada and around the world, to build and strengthen their workforce.
\n Contact Workforce Development if you are an employer who:
\n \n wants to participate in recruitment events or missions
\n is experiencing workforce shortages
\n needs information on workforce supports or immigration pathways
\n have questions about recruitment or employer engagement opportunities
\n \n If you are a candidate looking to connect with an employer from New Brunswick:
\n \n review the calendar below to learn more about upcoming events
