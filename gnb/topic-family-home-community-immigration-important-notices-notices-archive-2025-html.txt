 Endorsement application – Candidate pool system introduced
 Effective February 3, 2026 
\n Due to reductions across Canada’s immigration system, New Brunswick is implementing a candidate pool system for endorsement applications under the  Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP) . This allows for better management of our application inventory and for improved responsiveness to the provincial labour market needs. 
\n What you need to know 
\n \n designated employers and their candidates can continue to submit endorsement applications
\n these new applications will be placed in a pool
\n applications in the pool will be selected monthly for processing
\n selections will be based on provincial priorities, not on a first-come, first-served basis
\n applications not selected will expire after 365 days
\n \n If an application expires without being selected 
\n \n it will be removed from the pool
\n a new and complete endorsement application would be required for further consideration

 Effective February 3, 2026 
\n Immigration New Brunswick is not considering any endorsement applications under the Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP) for positions in the  accommodation and food services  sector (NAICS 72). This restriction includes, but is not limited to, the following NOC codes:
\n \n NOC 60030  – Restaurant and food service managers
\n NOC 62020  – Food service supervisors
\n NOC 62021 – Executive housekeepers
\n NOC 62024  – Cleaning supervisors
\n NOC 62200  – Chefs
\n NOC 63200  – Cooks
\n NOC 63202  – Bakers
\n NOC 64300  – Maîtres d’hôtel and hosts/hostesses
\n NOC 64301 – Bartenders
\n NOC 64314  – Hotel front desk clerks
\n \n However, designated employers who are not directly in the accommodation or food service sector (NAICS 72) may still submit an endorsement application to Immigration New Brunswick for these types of jobs.
\n Additionally, Immigration New Brunswick is not considering any endorsement applications for the following occupations, regardless of sectors:
\n \n NOC 14400  – Shippers and receivers
\n NOC 60020  – Retail and wholesale trade managers
\n NOC 62010  – Retail sales supervisors
\n NOC 62101  – Retail and wholesale buyers
\n NOC 64100  – Retail salespersons and visual merchandisers

\n \n In addition, endorsement applications for any positions offered to foreign nationals residing outside of Canada are limited to recruitment initiatives led by the Government of New Brunswick in:
\n \n health care
\n education
\n construction
\n \n If you are an employer in one of these sectors and would like more information about government-led recruitment options, contact&nbsp; WorkingNB .

 Private Career College Graduate Pilot reaches limited capacity
 The Private Career College Graduate Pilot is a 3-year pilot that launched in September 2022. Once the pilot closes, no new nominations will be issued.
\r\n
\r\n The pilot will remain open in a limited capacity through to the end of 2025. Please consult the program guide for details. 
\r\n
\r\nStudents who do not qualify for the Pilot or who will graduate after the pilot closure date are encouraged to review other New Brunswick provincial immigration programs and streams. Graduates with qualifying work experience and job offer from a New Brunswick employer may be eligible to apply when they complete their studies.

 The Private Career College Graduate Pilot is a 3-year pilot that launched in September 2022. Once the pilot closes, no new nominations will be issued.
 The pilot will remain open in a limited capacity through to the end of 2025. Please consult the program guide for details. 
Students who do not qualify for the Pilot or who will graduate after the pilot closure date are encouraged to review other New Brunswick provincial immigration programs and streams. Graduates with qualifying work experience and job offer from a New Brunswick employer may be eligible to apply when they complete their studies.
