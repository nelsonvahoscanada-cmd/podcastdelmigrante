Private occupational training certificate of registration 
 Private trainers offering employability programs must be registered to ensure student protection and uphold training standards.

 Private trainers offering employability programs must be registered to ensure student protection and uphold training standards.
 On this page
 Overview
 Private&nbsp;Trainers&nbsp;delivering&nbsp;programs to improve a person’s employability&nbsp;are required to register under the&nbsp; Private Occupational Training Act &nbsp;in order to receive a certificate of registration.&nbsp;Private Training Organizations&nbsp;are individuals who deliver&nbsp;programs&nbsp;for which&nbsp;tuition&nbsp;is collected.
\n"}}" id="text-33818309db" class="cmp-text">
 Private Trainers delivering programs to improve a person’s employability are required to register under the Private Occupational Training Act in order to receive a certificate of registration. Private Training Organizations are individuals who deliver programs for which tuition is collected.
 Requirements
 Private Training Organizations&nbsp;are required to&nbsp;register&nbsp;and&nbsp;provide Financial Security for the&nbsp;duration&nbsp;of&nbsp;the organization’s registration. All students enrolled in a registered program are&nbsp;required&nbsp;to pay a 1% Student Protection Fee, which must be&nbsp;submitted&nbsp;by the training organization along with the student list.
\n"}}" id="text-dbe158fd51" class="cmp-text">
 Private Training Organizations are required to register and provide Financial Security for the duration of the organization’s registration. All students enrolled in a registered program are required to pay a 1% Student Protection Fee, which must be submitted by the training organization along with the student list.
