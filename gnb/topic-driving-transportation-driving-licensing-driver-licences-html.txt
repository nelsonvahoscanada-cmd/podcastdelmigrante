 Other licensing information 
 In addition to a class, a driver's licence may be endorsed with a letter to expand on the types of vehicles that can be driven by the holder of the licence. The endorsements include: 
\r\n \r\n A - valid for all motorcycles &nbsp;
\r\n B - valid for school bus &nbsp;
\r\n D - valid for motorcycle with engine size exceeding 50 cubic centimetres, but not exceeding 550 cubic centimetres &nbsp;
\r\n E - valid for vehicles equipped with air brakes &nbsp;
\r\n
\r\n H - Graduated motorcycle licence up to 550 cc &nbsp;
\r\n I - Graduated motorcycle licence all motorcycles 
\r\n \r\n All newly licensed drivers must participate in the Graduated Licence Program. 
\r\n To qualify for a school bus endorsement a person must be at least 21 years of age and have one year of driving experience. 
\r\n When applying for a new licence or upgrading your current licence, testing will be required (some exceptions apply when exchanging a licence from another jurisdiction, please see Driver's Licences (new residents) . 
\r\n Test results are valid for six months. Any licence issuance or upgrade must be completed within six months of starting the process. 
\r\n The physical printing of Driver’s Licence and Identification Cards is done offsite at a facility that uses advanced security features. When applying for your licence you will receive a 30-day temporary document and your card will arrive securely in the mail within approximately 14 days. &nbsp;
\r\n
\r\n Photos are a mandatory feature on all New Brunswick driver's licences. To apply for a new driver's licence, please visit a Service New Brunswick Centre . A new photo will be required.

 In addition to a class, a driver's licence may be endorsed with a letter to expand on the types of vehicles that can be driven by the holder of the licence. The endorsements include: 
 A - valid for all motorcycles  
 B - valid for school bus  
 D - valid for motorcycle with engine size exceeding 50 cubic centimetres, but not exceeding 550 cubic centimetres  
 E - valid for vehicles equipped with air brakes  
 H - Graduated motorcycle licence up to 550 cc  
 I - Graduated motorcycle licence all motorcycles 
 All newly licensed drivers must participate in the Graduated Licence Program. 
 To qualify for a school bus endorsement a person must be at least 21 years of age and have one year of driving experience. 
 When applying for a new licence or upgrading your current licence, testing will be required (some exceptions apply when exchanging a licence from another jurisdiction, please see Driver's Licences (new residents) . 
 Test results are valid for six months. Any licence issuance or upgrade must be completed within six months of starting the process. 
 The physical printing of Driver’s Licence and Identification Cards is done offsite at a facility that uses advanced security features. When applying for your licence you will receive a 30-day temporary document and your card will arrive securely in the mail within approximately 14 days.  
 Photos are a mandatory feature on all New Brunswick driver's licences. To apply for a new driver's licence, please visit a Service New Brunswick Centre . A new photo will be required.
