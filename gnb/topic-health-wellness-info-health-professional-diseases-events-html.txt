 Overview
 The New Brunswick Notifiable Diseases and Events Guide provides policies, program standards and guidelines for Regional Public Health in the prevention and control of reportable diseases and events. The guide is intended to be used in conjunction with other resources, including national practice standards, scientific literature, evidence-informed practice and expert advice from Medical Officers of Health.&nbsp;
\n The Guide is updated regularly to reflect changes in evidence and resources.&nbsp;

 The New Brunswick Notifiable Diseases and Events Guide provides policies, program standards and guidelines for Regional Public Health in the prevention and control of reportable diseases and events. The guide is intended to be used in conjunction with other resources, including national practice standards, scientific literature, evidence-informed practice and expert advice from Medical Officers of Health. 
 The Guide is updated regularly to reflect changes in evidence and resources.
