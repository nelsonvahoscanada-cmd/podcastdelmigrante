 The New Brunswick Business Immigration stream is for entrepreneurs who want to start or buy a business in New Brunswick.
 The entrepreneur must run the business and take part in its daily management. If requirements are met, the province may nominate them for permanent residence.
 Eligibility
 To be eligible, you must:
\n \n be 19 to 59 years old
\n have at least a level 4 in all four language skills according to the Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB)
\n have at least Canadian high school diploma or its foreign equivalent
\n have a personal net worth of at least $500,000 (or $300,000 if you are investing in the agriculture sector)
\n have two years of qualifying experience within the last five years:
\n&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-&nbsp; &nbsp; own a private company (51% or more) or be a senior manager in a for-profit business
\n&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;manage daily operations
\n&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;supervise at least two employees
\n have a business plan:
\n&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;with at least $150,000 in eligible investments
\n&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;that create at least one full-time job
\n 65 out of 100 points on the selection factor grid
\n \n Read the New Brunswick Business Immigration stream application guide (PDF 392 KB) .

 To be eligible, you must:
 be 19 to 59 years old
 have at least a level 4 in all four language skills according to the Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB)
 have at least Canadian high school diploma or its foreign equivalent
 have a personal net worth of at least $500,000 (or $300,000 if you are investing in the agriculture sector)
 have two years of qualifying experience within the last five years:
 - own a private company (51% or more) or be a senior manager in a for-profit business
 - manage daily operations
 - supervise at least two employees
 have a business plan:
 - with at least $150,000 in eligible investments
 - that create at least one full-time job
 65 out of 100 points on the selection factor grid
 Read the New Brunswick Business Immigration stream application guide (PDF 392 KB) .
 Apply
 \n Review the application guide to make sure you meet program requirements
\n Review important information for every candidate 
\n Submit an expression of interest 
\n Wait for an invitation to apply 
\n If you are invited, submit a complete application
\n If your application is approved, apply for a work permit
\n Start your business in New Brunswick
\n&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-&nbsp; &nbsp; Within one month of arrival, report your arrival (NBBIS-004)
\n&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;Within three months of arrival, you may ask to update your business plan (NBBIS-007), subject to approval
\n&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;Within nine months of arrival, report your business opening (NBBIS-005)
\n After six months of business operations per the Business Performance Agreement, ask for a nomination (NBBIS-006)
\n Apply for permanent residence with the nomination certificate
\n \n Submitting an expression of interest is free of charge. However, before submitting a complete nomination application, you must pay a non-refundable application fee of $2,000.
