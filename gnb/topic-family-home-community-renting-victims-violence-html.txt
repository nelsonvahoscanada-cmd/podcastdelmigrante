 Victims of gender-based violence and criminal harassment may end their lease early for their safety or the safety of their children.
 On this page
 Overview
 Victims of gender-based violence or criminal harassment may end their lease early for their safety or the safety of their children without the financial impact of breaking a lease agreement. This includes victims of domestic violence, intimate partner violence, sexual violence, or criminal harassment (stalking).

 Victims of gender-based violence or criminal harassment may end their lease early for their safety or the safety of their children without the financial impact of breaking a lease agreement. This includes victims of domestic violence, intimate partner violence, sexual violence, or criminal harassment (stalking).
 How it works
 Victims of gender-based violence or criminal harassment in a year-to-year lease or a fixed-term lease may end their lease early for their safety or the safety of their children without the financial impact of breaking a lease agreement. In these circumstances, the notice period is reduced to one month instead of three&nbsp; months.
\r\n If a tenant needs to end their lease early for safety reasons, they must provide one full rental month written notice to their landlord.
\r\n For example, if the rent is due on the 1st of the month, you must give notice before the 1st of the month to be effective for the last day of that month.

In these circumstances, the notice period is reduced to one month instead of three months.
 If a tenant needs to end their lease early for safety reasons, they must provide one full rental month written notice to their landlord.
 For example, if the rent is due on the 1st of the month, you must give notice before the 1st of the month to be effective for the last day of that month. 
 If your landlord requests documentation
 If your landlord asks you for documentation, you may provide them with one of the following:
\n \n an emergency intervention order
\n an order of the court
\n a signed Third-Party Declaration
\n \n You can get a printed copy of the Third-Party Declaration form at any Service New Brunswick service centre , or you can download and print the form yourself.
