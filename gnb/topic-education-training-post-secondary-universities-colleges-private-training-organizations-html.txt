 Registered under the New Brunswick Private Occupational Training Act. 
 Overview
 Registration under the New Brunswick Private Occupational Training Act does not attest to the quality of the occupational training programs or the instructional staff of the training organization.
\n The Post-Secondary Relations Branch does not retain any student records such as diplomas or certificates, transcripts or marks, or any proof of attendance at private institutions that are no longer registered under the Private Occupational Training Act due to a closure.

 Registration under the New Brunswick Private Occupational Training Act does not attest to the quality of the occupational training programs or the instructional staff of the training organization.
 The Post-Secondary Relations Branch does not retain any student records such as diplomas or certificates, transcripts or marks, or any proof of attendance at private institutions that are no longer registered under the Private Occupational Training Act due to a closure.
