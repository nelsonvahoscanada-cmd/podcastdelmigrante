COW – 33K + Ongoing, One-time Initiative—Temporary Resident to Permanent Resident transition – May 28, 2026
The Initiative
Budget 2025 announced we would speed up the transition of 33,000 workers to permanent residence in 2026 and 2027.
This is not a new program. We have been clear about its scope and intent from the beginning.
We are selecting workers who:
have already applied for permanent residence in regional programs and occupation-driven pilots; and
have been living in smaller Canadian communities for 2 years or more.
This initiative will help meet the labour needs of smaller communities with workers who are already established and contributing to our economy.
This initiative is not intended for undocumented or out-of-status individuals.
Progress
3,600 workers were granted permanent residence since February 28, 2026.
