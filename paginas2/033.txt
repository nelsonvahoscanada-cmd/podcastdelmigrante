 Staying in Québec after your studies
 Obtaining a post-graduation work permit
 If you wish to work in Québec after completing your studies, you may be eligible to apply for a post-graduation work permit with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). 
 The validity period of your post-graduation work permit depends on the length of your study program. It allows you to work for any employer in Canada.
 Changes were recently made to the eligibility conditions for post-graduation work permits for international students. Please note that the choice of study program determines if, at the time you successfully complete your studies, you can submit an application for a PGWP. Consult the page Applying to an educational institution for relevant information regarding your study program.
 Costs and time
 Visit the IRCC website for post-graduation work permit fees and processing times .
 Immigration programs for skilled workers
 If you want to stay in Quebec permanently after your studies, visit the Immigrating to Quebec Permanently page for more information.
