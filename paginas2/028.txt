 International Mobility Program
 The International Mobility Program of the Government of Canada allows an employer to hire a temporary foreign worker without having to submit a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) request.
 In most cases, an employer who wants to hire you under this program must submit an offer of employment through the Employer Portal . You will have to attach the number of your offer of employment to your work permit application .
 Once your work permit application is approved, you will receive:
 A letter of introduction, if you are outside of Canada; or
 A new work permit, if you are already in Canada or if you submit your permit application when you enter Canada.
 If you receive a letter of introduction, you will have to present it upon your arrival in Canada to obtain a work permit issued by the Canada Border Services officer, as the case may be.
 If your employer offers you a job under the International Mobility Program, you do not have to submit an application under the Temporary Foreign Worker Program . The Temporary Foreign Worker Program (TFWP) is designed for the hiring of temporary foreign workers who need an LMIA.
