Claiming asylum in Canada

Start a claim from inside Canada

We’ve made changes to the Canadian asylum system Nov 3, 2026

If you already submitted a claim, we’ll process it as usual.

If you started a claim in the IRCC Portal before November 3 but didn’t submit it:

you’ll need to answer new questions and review old ones
your personal information has been saved

Learn more about the updates to our asylum system.

If you’re in Canada, you need to send us some information and documents in the IRCC Portal first. We’ll then invite you to an in-person interview where:

you can officially make your asylum claim to an officer
we check if your claim is eligible
Prepare your documents

Get a scanner or camera ready to make copies of your documents.

You and each family member claiming asylum will need to upload a copy of a passport, travel document or other identification document.

If you’re getting help with your application from someone else
a) Gather personal evidence

You can upload documents that prove you need protection from your home country. This is called personal evidence.

We won’t use it to decide if your claim eligible at this time, but we’ll ask for the documents later and some can take a longer to find or get.

If your claim is eligible, you can keep uploading personal evidence when you complete your claim.

Learn more about collecting evidence.

Create an IRCC Portal account

Use one account for you and any family members wanting to claim asylum at the same time.

You can’t change the email address associated with your account once you sign up, so make sure you have continued access to the email account.

Get an IRCC portal account

If you can’t apply online and need accommodations
If you’re applying with family
Start your claim in the IRCC Portal

Sign in to your IRCC Portal account and select Make a new refugee claim or continue a claim made to the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA).

We’ll ask you to:

enter information about yourself and any family members claiming asylum with you
answer questions about your backgrounds
upload required documents, depending on your answers

Answer all questions truthfully—it’s a serious offence to provide false or misleading information on your application.

If you need help, use the application guide.

If your application is complete, we’ll send you instructions and a date for your eligibility interview.

If we need more information or documents, we’ll message you in the IRCC Portal to let you know:

what you need to send us
the deadline for sending it

If you don’t send us the missing information on time, we’ll close your file. Because we haven’t made a decision on the eligibility of your claim, you can reapply in the future.

Go to an eligibility interview

The eligibility interview is where:

you officially make your asylum claim to an officer
they decide if your claim is eligible



Don’t miss your interview. If you miss it, we’ll close your file. Because we haven’t made a decision on the eligibility of your claim, you can reapply in the future.

What to bring to your interview

Everyone on your application must bring:

original passports and travel documents
any other identification documents you may have
What happens at your interview

An immigration officer will ask you questions about who you are and the information you sent us. The officer will also:

seize the original travel documents and give you certified copies
fingerprint everyone age 14 and older
photograph every family member
After your interview

At the end of the interview, the immigration officer will tell you whether your claim is eligible.
