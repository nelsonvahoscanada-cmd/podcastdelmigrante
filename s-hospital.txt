 Find a hospital or health centre 
 Find a hospital or health centre address, phone number, opening times and list of services.
 Sexual or reproductive health support
 The [https://www.facebook.com/pg/YSHCwhitehorse/about/?ref=page_internal] Yukon Sexual Health Clinic is a private clinic that offers sexual and reproductive health services to all genders and orientations. You do not need a referral from another health-care provider.
 Location: Cranberry Health at 602 Ogilvie Street in Whitehorse.
 Booked appointments: 
 Mondays between 9 am and 4 pm
 Tuesdays between 1 pm and 7 pm
 Phone: 867-393-6635
 Menopause health support
 The [https://www.facebook.com/YWMHCwhitehorse/] Yukon Women’s MidLife Health Clinic is a specialty clinic that offers menopause-related health care and support to women. This includes women who are experiencing perimenopause or are beyond menopause.
 You do not need a referral from another health-care provider.
 They are located in the Pine Medical Centre at 5110 5th Avenue in Whitehorse.
 Phone: 867-633-3080
 Hours of operation: 9 am to 4 pm
