 Find sailing times for Yukon ferries 
 The Government of Yukon operates 2 ferries in the territory. Both ferries are free.
 Find information on the:
 [#george-black-ferry] George Black ferry in Dawson ; and
 [#pelly-barge] Pelly Barge ferry in Ross River .
 You can find the most up-to-date information on both ferries using [https://yukon.ca/en/driving-and-transportation/travel-yukon/find-road-conditions-511yukon] 511 Yukon .
 George Black ferry in Dawson
 This ferry connects the North Klondike Highway and the Top of the World Highway across the Yukon River.
 Status
 The ferry is now open for the season.
 Schedule
 The ferry runs from mid-May to mid-October, depending on weather conditions. Service runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, though hours may be adjusted near the season’s end based on conditions. 
 How to use the ferry
 Users line up on a 1st-come, 1st-served basis. There are no reservations.
 You can ride the ferry on foot, bicycle or in your vehicle.
 A crossing takes 6 to 7 minutes.
 You may have to wait slightly longer in the morning and evening due to heavier traffic volumes.
 Size restrictions 
 RVs and other oversized vehicles can use the ferry. All oversized vehicles need approval to travel in the Yukon. Email [/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#054d5552284a73607772606c626d71457c706e6a6b2b6664] [email protected] to apply for a permit. 
The maximum dimensions for the George Black Ferry are: 
 Maximum weight: 65,000 kilograms (143,000 lbs) 
 Maximum length: 25 metres (83 feet) 
 Maximum width: 5 metres (17 feet) 
 Wait times for oversized vehicles may be longer depending on the demand. 
 Animals on the ferry
 You can bring animals on the ferry if they are confined or leashed. 
 Pelly Barge in Ross River
 This ferry connects the Canol Road across the Pelly River.
 Status
 The Pelly Barge ferry in Ross River is open for the season.
 Schedule
 The ferry runs from early June to the 2nd Tuesday of October. 
 The opening and closing of the ferry depends on:
 weather conditions; and
 the water levels of the Pelly River.
 Restrictions 
RVs and other oversized vehicles can use the ferry. All oversized vehicles need approval to travel in the Yukon. Email [/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#eea6beb9c3a1988b9c998b8789869aae979b858180c08d8f] [email protected] to apply for a permit. 
The maximum dimensions for the Pelly Barge are: 
• Maximum weight: 25,000 kilograms (55,115 lbs) 
• Maximum length: 21.3 metres (70 feet) 
• Maximum width: 3.96 metres (13 feet) 
Wait times for oversized vehicles may be longer depending on the demand.
