 Discover the fare types that cover most trip needs: the All Modes fare, the Bus fare, as well as Unlimited Evening and Unlimited Weekend passes. 
 Other fares will remain available. Refer to the fare schedule to see all fares available. 
 Select your fare
 Based on your trip
 Your fare must be valid in the zone you are departing from, the zones you will travel through and the zone you will arrive in. Not sure which zone you’ll be travelling through? Find out more about zones A, B, C and D . 
 Based on your mode of transportation
 Choose between the following fares to access the public transit services you use: bus, train, metro or the REM.

 All Modes
 The All Modes fare allows you to take the metro, REM, train and bus. You may choose between All Modes A, AB, ABC and ABCD fares. 
 Learn more 
 Bus
 Bus fares give you access to bus services only. You may choose between the Bus fare and the Out-of-Territory Bus fare. 
 Learn more 
 Unlimited Evening and Unlimited Weekend
 The Unlimited Evening and Unlimited Weekend passes allow unlimited travel in zones A, B, C and D, regardless of the public transit mode you choose. The more you use your pass, the more you save! 
 Learn more 
 Free at all times for children
 Children aged 11 and under can travel anywhere for free at all times on Public transit services while under the supervision of a person aged 14 and up. 
 This person must have a valid and validated transit pass and may not accompany more than five children aged 6 to 11.
