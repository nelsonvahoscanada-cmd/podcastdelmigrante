 Free transit for kids and seniors 
 Public transit is free for kids aged 11 and under as long as they’re accompanied by someone 14 or over who’s responsible for them. It’s also free for Montréal residents aged 65 and over .

 Frequently asked questions 
 How do I prove I paid my fare during a fare check? 
 Your validated fare is your proof of payment . Keep it with you at all times during your trip and show it to inspectors if asked. 
 If you’re using paratransit , your proof of payment will be recorded on the driver’s trip sheet . STM inspectors may check this while you’re in the vehicle. 
 If you don’t have proof of payment, you could face fines of up to $500 (not including fees).
