 Fares 
 To ride the bus in Laval, you need to purchase bus fares. 
All Modes fares give you more flexibility when travelling between Laval and Montréal (train, bus, metro and REM). 
 Which fare should you choose? 
 To help you find the fare that best suits your needs, the Autorité régionale de transport métropolitain (ARTM) offers a fare selector . 
Simply enter a few details about your planned trip, and you’re all set.

 Free transit for kids! 
 Public transit is free for children aged 0 to 5, as well as for children aged 6 to 11 when accompanied by someone 14 or older who is responsible for them. 
 The accompanying person must have a valid fare and can travel with up to 5 children. 
 If your child travels alone, make sure to get an OPUS card with photo to purchase reduced fares. 
 Learn more about the OPUS card with photo
