 Portal for the recognition of competencies
 Welcome to the Québec’s portal for the recognition of competencies !
 Please, note that our website is mainly in french. However, we offer services in french, english and spanish.
 Thanks to our search engine, you can access all the information about all professions and how to proceed to recognize your competencies and exercise in Québec.
 Search for your profession 
 If you have questions about the recognition of your competencies, or if you need support for processus, you can reach us here . Our services are available and free wherever you are.
