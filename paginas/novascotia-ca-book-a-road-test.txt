Book a Road Test
 You can now book all classes of road tests online in Nova Scotia. 
 Booking a road test is the next step to getting your Nova Scotia driver's licence. Check below to see if you are eligible to book a road test online.

 Who should not book a road test online?
 you do not need to book a road test if you are eligible to exchange your licence from any province in Canada, or another location listed here 
 Note: if you have a valid out of country driver's licence from a non-reciprocal country, visit the Exchange Your Non-Nova Scotian Driver's Licence page for more information on how to book a road test and exchange your licence. 
 if you have an incomplete or unsuccessful road test, you must wait until the next day before you can book another road test
 we will do what we can to make adjustments for medical, cultural or religious needs, as the law allows. To make a booking with adjustment needs, call:

 Before you take your test: Required Information and Documents
 Vehicles are not provided for the road test. You must bring a vehicle you will use for the road test.
 Make sure to bring the following documents to the road test:
 valid driver's licence or valid out of country driver's licence
 documents for the vehicle you will use for your road test:
 proof of motor vehicle liability insurance 
 valid vehicle permit or temporary permit
 proof of valid motor vehicle inspection
 certificate of completion from a recognized Driver Training School (if applicable)
 a Driver's Medical Exam Report completed in the last 6 months if you are booking an appointment for a Class 1, 2, 2B, 3 or 4 licence
 Before the road test, a Safety Check of your vehicle will be conducted even if you have a valid motor vehicle inspection.
 How to pay for your road test
 When you book your test online, you can choose to pay with one of these options:
 Option 1: Pay online with a credit card (recommended) 
 Use the new Book a Road Test payment tool to pay securely with a credit card. Once you start the booking process below, you'll be guided to the payment page.
 Option 2: Pay in-person 
 Visit any Access Nova Scotia or Registry of Motor Vehicles to pay with cash, debit, cheque or other non-credit card methods. You'll receive a road test receipt number to enter during booking.
 The cost for the road test for all classes is $52.54 (HST included). The cost for an interview is $68.10 (HST included).
 How to book your road test if you have already paid
 Have you already paid for your road test?
 if you paid in-person or paid using the Pay for a Road test service and have not booked your road test yet, start the booking process below, and enter your road test receipt number when prompted
 if you've already paid and booked, you can reschedule your road test online 
 Do you want to pay for your road test now and book later?
 visit the Pay for a Road Test Receipt page to pay online and receive a road test receipt number, which you can enter later when you're ready to book your road test online 
 Clear any fines or fees before booking
 Make sure all outstanding fines or reinstatement fees are paid before booking your road test. You will not be able to receive or upgrade your driver’s licence after the test unless they are paid.
