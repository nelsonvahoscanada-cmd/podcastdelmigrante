 About ISANS 
 ISANS is a non-profit organization empowering immigrants and refugees to build their futures in Nova Scotia. With over 45 years of experience, we support more than 14,500 newcomers from 170+ countries each year as Atlantic Canada’s leading settlement agency.
 We offer a wide range of services, including language services , settlement support for families and children , employment programs , business development , community integration , and pre-arrival services , delivered in person and online in English and 46+ other languages.
 ISANS is also committed to supporting Canada’s Francophone Integration Pathway and champions diversity, accessibility, and support for equity-deserving communities.
