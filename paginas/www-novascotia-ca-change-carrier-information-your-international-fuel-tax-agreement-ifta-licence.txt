Change the carrier information on your International Fuel Tax Agreement (IFTA) Licence
 Interjurisdictional carriers registered in Nova Scotia need to use the International Fuel Tax Agreement (IFTA) Request to Change Carrier Information Form to change the information on their IFTA Licence.
 Change or update the carrier information on your IFTA Licence.
 Who can use this form
 Interjurisdictional carriers registered in Nova Scotia need to use this form to change or update the carrier information on their IFTA Licence, including:
 business name
 Business Number from Canada Revenue Agency (CRA)
 civic or mailing address
 business contact person
 What you need to do
 Complete the form online.
 Check the form for details on all required supporting documents.
 Submit your completed form and any supporting documents.
 How long it takes
 It should take 2 weeks to get your revised IFTA Licence. It can take longer if more information is needed or if your form hasn’t been filled in correctly.
 Cost
 There is no cost to submit the form.
 Before you start
 Make sure you have:
 your IFTA account number
 copy of name change documentation from Registry of Joint Stock Companies if you’re changing your business name
 copy of your Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Business Number if you have a new business number
 International Fuel Tax Agreement Letter of Authority Form if you have a new business contact person
 Online
 When you submit the form online you need to create an account or sign in. You can’t access the form online if you’re outside North America or your computer’s IP address is outside North America.
 Start now 
 Other ways to submit the form
 You can use the International Fuel Tax Agreement (IFTA) Request to Change Carrier Information Form (PDF 243 kB) if you’re unable to submit the form online. Send your completed application and any supporting documents by mail. Or drop it off at the Business Registration Unit.
