 Québec Handy Numbers
 Subscribe to this content 
 Did you know that the household savings rate is 8.2% or that people aged 65 and over make up slightly more than 20% of the population? These data are a preview of what you will find in the 2026 edition of Le Québec chiffres en main. 
 Every year, this practical, easy-to-read booklet summarizes key statistical data on Québec and its regions. It provides an overview of the province’s main socio-economic indicators and is structured around four major themes: territory, population, living conditions, and the economy and finance. Detailed data for each of Québec’s 17 administrative regions round off the booklet, thereby providing a better understanding of the specific characteristics and realities of each region.
