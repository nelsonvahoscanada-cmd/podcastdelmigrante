Agreement to end tenancy (lease) for demolition, repairs or renovations (Form DR5)
 Landlords and tenants use Form DR5 to end a tenancy (lease) for demolition, repairs or renovations to a residential premises when both parties (tenant and landlord) agree.
 Use Form DR5: Agreement to Terminate for Demolition, Repairs or Renovations if both parties (tenant and landlord) agree to end a tenancy (lease) for demolition, repairs or renovations.
 Before you start
 Make sure you have:
 tenant’s name and address
 landlord’s name and address
 date tenancy ends
 compensation amount
 information about the tenant accepting another unit from the landlord, if applicable
 number of units in the building
 Who can use this form
 Landlords and tenants need to use this form to end a tenancy (lease) for demolition, repairs or renovations when both parties (tenant and landlord) agree.
 What you need to do
 Complete the form.
 Both parties (tenant and landlord) sign the form.
 Keep a copy of the form for your records.
