Accessible journeys matter 
 Accessible transit means more than just physical accessibility. It means freedom, independence and connection to community. 
 Winter 2027 recreation registration dates
 Recreation program registration opens on Dec. 1. Aquatic and skating program registration opens on Dec. 8. 
 Hurricane season awareness
 June through November is hurricane season. Be prepared: learn how to make an emergency plan, a home emergency kit and how to stay connected in case you lose power.
 Halifax Christmas Tree Lighting
 On Saturday, Nov. 28, enjoy the lighting of the municipal Christmas tree, PAW Patrol Skye & Chase, Gabby's Dollhouse dance party, live music by the Julia Tynes band, a visit from Santa Claus and fireworks.
 Upcoming traffic changes are coming to Bayers Road
 Construction is underway for Phase 2 of the Bayers Road Transit Priority Corridor project. 
 Opportunity for artists - creation of indoor public artwork
 Let your art become part of the Eastern Shore's story. Submit your public art proposal by Jan. 11, 2027.
 Volunteer with the African Descent Advisory Committee (ADAC)
 The ADAC advises Regional Council, through Executive Standing Committee, on the impact of municipal policies, programs and services for People of African Descent. Learn more about open roles and apply by Dec. 7 at halifax.ca/serve
 A seat at the table
 Volunteer to serve on a municipal board, committee or commission. Learn more and apply by Dec. 7 at halifax.ca/serve.
 Centre Plan 2026 updates
 The Halifax Regional Municipality is inviting residents to review and provide feedback on proposed updates to the Centre Plan.
