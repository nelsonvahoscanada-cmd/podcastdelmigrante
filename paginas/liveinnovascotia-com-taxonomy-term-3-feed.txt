<span class="field field--name-created field--type-created field--label-hidden"><time datetime="2026-08-06T11:14:41-03:00" title="Thursday, August 6, 2026 - 11:14" class="datetime">Thu, 08/06/2026 - 11:14</time>

 <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p>August 6, 2026</p><p>Effective&nbsp;September 1, 2026, Nova Scotia will introduce application fees for the Nova Scotia Nominee Program (NSNP).&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Why Fees Are Being Introduced</strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;</h2><p>Application&nbsp;fees will help ensure that&nbsp;the NSNP continues to&nbsp;operate&nbsp;efficiently and with strong program&nbsp;integrity&nbsp;by:&nbsp;</p><ul><li>Supporting&nbsp;effective program administration and&nbsp;assessment&nbsp;processes&nbsp;&nbsp;</li><li>Helping ensure&nbsp;limited nomination spaces are&nbsp;allocated&nbsp;in support of Nova Scotia’s labour market and economic priorities&nbsp;</li><li>Recovering a&nbsp;portion&nbsp;of the administrative costs associated with assessing applications&nbsp;</li></ul><p>Introducing fees aligns&nbsp;Nova Scotia with&nbsp;most&nbsp;jurisdictions&nbsp;across&nbsp;Canada.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>When Fees Apply</strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;</h2><p>Fees will only be charged&nbsp;once&nbsp;a candidate has been selected from the expression of interest&nbsp;(EOI)&nbsp;pool&nbsp;for assessment.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><ul><li>There is no fee to&nbsp;submit&nbsp;an EOI.&nbsp;&nbsp;</li><li>Any EOIs selected for assessment on or after&nbsp;September 1,&nbsp;2026&nbsp;are subject to&nbsp;the&nbsp;application fee, regardless of when the EOI was&nbsp;submitted.&nbsp;&nbsp;</li><li>Candidates who received their selection letter before&nbsp;September 1,&nbsp;2026 will not be required to pay the fee.&nbsp;&nbsp;</li></ul><p>This change does not affect the Atlantic Immigration Program&nbsp;(AIP).&nbsp;The AIP will continue to operate without a provincial application fee, consistent&nbsp;with other Atlantic provinces.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Application&nbsp;Fee Amount</strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;</h2><p>Effective&nbsp;September 1, 2026, the following application fees apply:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><ul><li>$1,000&nbsp;for&nbsp;the&nbsp;NSNP worker streams (Skilled Worker, Nova Scotia Graduate, Nova Scotia:&nbsp;Express Entry)&nbsp;&nbsp;</li><li>$2,000&nbsp;for&nbsp;the NSNP&nbsp;entrepreneur stream&nbsp;&nbsp;</li></ul><p>&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Payment Requirements</strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;</h2><ul><li>Application&nbsp;fees are&nbsp;non-refundable&nbsp;except where required by law or&nbsp;when&nbsp;an&nbsp;administrative error has occurred.&nbsp;&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li><li>Application fees deadline:&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;<ul><li><strong>Worker streams (Skilled Worker, Nova Scotia Experience: Express Entry, and Nova Scotia Graduate):</strong> The application fee must be paid within 7 calendar days of receiving notification that your EOI has been selected for assessment.&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li><li><strong>Entrepreneur stream:</strong><ul><li><strong>Entrepreneur stream – candidates starting a new business:</strong> The application fee must be paid within 90 calendar days of receiving an Invitation to Apply.&nbsp;</li><li><strong>Entrepreneur stream – candidates&nbsp;purchasing&nbsp;an existing business: </strong>The application fee must be paid within 180 calendar days of receiving an Invitation to Apply.&nbsp;</li><li><strong>Entrepreneur stream – International&nbsp;Graduates: </strong>The application fee must be paid within 90 calendar days of receiving an Invitation to Apply.&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li></ul></li></ul></li><li>Failure to provide proof of payment within&nbsp;the&nbsp;specified&nbsp;timeline&nbsp;will result in&nbsp;the&nbsp;closure of the&nbsp;EOI&nbsp;and the submission will not&nbsp;proceed&nbsp;to assessment.&nbsp;Candidates may&nbsp;submit&nbsp;a new EOI&nbsp;if they continue to meet eligibility&nbsp;requirements.&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li></ul><h2><strong>Supporting a Stronger Immigration System</strong>&nbsp;&nbsp;</h2><p>Nova Scotia&nbsp;remains&nbsp;committed to attracting and&nbsp;retaining&nbsp;newcomers who intend to settle, work, and build their lives in the province, while continuing to strengthen and modernize its immigration programs.&nbsp;</p><h2><br><strong>NSNP Application Fees - Frequently Asked Questions</strong></h2><h3><strong>Q: Why are fees being introduced now?</strong></h3><p>Demand for nominations continues to exceed Nova Scotia’s annual federal allocation. Introducing an application fee will help support the sustainability and effective administration of the program and aligns Nova Scotia with most other&nbsp;provincial immigration programs.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: When do the fees take effect?</strong>&nbsp;</h3><p>The fees take effect on September 1, 2026.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Who will need to pay the fee?</strong>&nbsp;</h3><p>NSNP Candidates&nbsp;whose Expression of Interest (EOI) is selected for assessment&nbsp;on or after&nbsp; September 1, 2026&nbsp;will need&nbsp;to pay the applicable fee, regardless of EOI submission date.&nbsp;There is no fee to submit an EOI.&nbsp;</p><p>Candidates who&nbsp;received their selection letter before&nbsp; September 1, 2026 are not required&nbsp;to pay the fee.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: At what stage in the process should I pay the fee?</strong></h3><p>If your EOI is selected for assessment, you will receive instructions by email on how to pay the application fee. There is no fee associated with submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI).</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Is the fee refundable?&nbsp;</strong></h3><p>Application fees are non-refundable.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: How much is the fee?</strong></h3><p>The application fee depends on the stream you are selected for assessment under:</p><ul><li>$1,000 for worker streams: Nova Scotia: Skilled Worker, Nova Scotia: Graduate, and Nova Scotia: Express Entry.</li><li>$2,000 for the Entrepreneur stream.</li></ul><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Does having dependents affect the fee amount?</strong></h3><p>No, dependents do not affect the fee. The fee is charged per principal applicant and does not change based on the number of dependents included in the application.</p><h3><br><strong>Q: How can I make the payment? What methods are accepted?</strong></h3><p>We accept the following methods of payment: Visa, MasterCard, Discover Card and American Express.</p><p>Payment must be made online, in a single payment.&nbsp;</p><p>If your EOI is selected for assessment, you will receive instructions on how to pay the application fee.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: How much time will I have to submit the payment once I am invited?</strong></h3><ul><li><strong>Worker streams (Skilled Worker, Nova Scotia Experience: Express Entry, and Nova Scotia Graduate): </strong>The application fee must be paid within 7 calendar days of receiving notification that your EOI has been selected for assessment.<br>&nbsp;</li><li><strong>Entrepreneur stream:</strong><ul><li><strong>Entrepreneur stream – candidates starting a new business: </strong>The application fee must be paid within 90 calendar days of receiving an Invitation to Apply.</li><li><strong>Entrepreneur stream – candidates purchasing an existing business:</strong> The application fee must be paid within 180 calendar days of receiving an Invitation to Apply.</li><li><strong>Entrepreneur stream – International Graduates: </strong>The application fee must be paid within 90 calendar days of receiving an Invitation to Apply.</li></ul></li></ul><p>Applications that remain unpaid after the deadline will not be processed.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Will there be exemptions or fee waivers?&nbsp;</strong></h3><p>No. The application fee applies to all NSNP streams and all applicants selected for assessment. Fee waivers and exemptions are not available.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Once I have paid, what happens next?</strong></h3><p>If you have submitted all required documents and paid the fees within the timeframe provided, your submission will proceed to assessment. Once the assessment is complete, you will receive a decision on your submission.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Will I receive a confirmation or receipt relating to my payment of the application fee?</strong></h3><p>Yes, you will receive a receipt by email. Keep this proof of payment as you will be required to submit the proof of payment to us. A screenshot of the confirmation page will not be accepted as proof of payment.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Once I have paid the fee, am I guaranteed to be nominated?</strong></h3><p>No. &nbsp;Paying the application fee does not affect the assessment outcome or increase the likelihood of nomination.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Does paying the fee result in faster processing?</strong></h3><p>No. Payment of the application fee does not impact processing times. All submissions are processed based on program priorities and operational capacity.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: If I reapply, do I need to pay the fee again?</strong></h3><p>Application fees are non‑refundable. If you choose to submit a new Expression of Interest (EOI) and are selected again for the NSNP, you will be required to pay the application fee again.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Can I pre-pay the fee while I wait to be selected?</strong></h3><p>No. You can only pay the fee if your EOI submission is selected for assessment.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Can I pay the fee in installments?&nbsp;</strong></h3><p>No. The fee must be paid in the full amount if your EOI is selected for assessment.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Can I pay the fee with different credit cards (e.g. $500 on one card, $500 on another)?</strong></h3><p>No. The fee must be paid in full using a single payment method.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q. What happens if my payment is declined?</strong></h3><p>A successful payment is required before your application can be accepted for assessment. If your payment is declined, your application will be considered incomplete and assessment will not begin until payment is received within the timeline provided. Applications that remain unpaid after the deadline will not be processed.</p><p>&nbsp;</p></div>

 <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p>July 14, 2026<br><br>Nova Scotia is implementing a one-time expansion of its immigration selection priorities to align with current labour market needs and available nomination spaces in 2026.&nbsp;</p><p>Nova Scotia continues to experience strong interest in its immigration programs while operating within limited federal immigration allocations. As a result, the Province must make strategic decisions about how nomination and endorsement spaces are used to support economic growth, workforce needs, and population retention.&nbsp;</p><p>Nova Scotia seeks to balance retaining individuals who are already living and working in the province and may have limited temporary status remaining with addressing the province's long-term labour market needs. This includes retaining workers who are contributing to Nova Scotia's economy today while continuing to support sectors and occupations experiencing persistent workforce shortages that cannot be filled through the local labour market.&nbsp;</p><p>Meeting eligibility requirements for a program does not guarantee selection, as the Province must also consider program integrity, available spaces, and broader labour market and economic priorities when selecting Expressions of Interest.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Limited Expansion of Selection Priorities</strong>&nbsp;</h2><p>Nova Scotia will continue to prioritize candidates in <a class="Hyperlink SCXW135197152 BCX8" href="https://liveinnovascotia.com/resources/nominee-program-priorities-nova-scotia" target="_blank">previously published priority occupations</a>.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p><strong>As a one-time initiative</strong>, the Province is expanding its selection from active EOIs submitted on or before June 30, 2026, to include additional candidates already living and working in Nova Scotia. This approach supports workforce retention and helps address labour market needs within Nova Scotia’s available nomination spaces.&nbsp;</p><p>The expanded selection includes candidates who have already submitted an EOI and who have a work permit expiry date in 2026 or earlier, and who meet one or more of the following criteria:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><ul><li>Working in a TEER 0-4 occupation that supports key sectors of Nova Scotia's economy, such as Professional &amp; Scientific, Manufacturing, Construction, Health &amp; Social Services, Natural Resources, Agriculture, and Transportation;&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li><li>Graduating from a Nova Scotia designated learning institution and working in a TEER 0-5 occupation;&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li><li>Living in a community outside the Halifax Regional Municipality and working in a TEER 0-5 occupation;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li><li>Working in a Sales and Service occupation (TEER 0-2) and earning at least $20 per hour at the time of EOI submission; or&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li><li>Working in Nova Scotia in any occupation and earning at least $27 per hour at the time of EOI submission.&nbsp;</li></ul><p>Work permit expiry may be considered as a secondary factor when determining the order in which selected applications are processed.&nbsp;</p><p>For international recruitment, Nova Scotia will continue to focus on occupations experiencing significant workforce shortages. This includes healthcare occupations, skilled trades, and selected occupations that support the delivery of critical services in Nova Scotia communities.&nbsp;</p><p>Candidates selected through this one-time initiative will be contacted directly by the Department. If you are not contacted, your existing EOI will remain in the pool and may be considered under the Province's previously published selection priorities, which remain in effect.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Priorities May Change Over Time</strong>&nbsp;</h2><p>Nova Scotia's immigration priorities are responsive to changing labour market conditions, workforce needs, and available immigration allocations. As these factors evolve, selection priorities may also change.&nbsp;</p><p>Candidates are encouraged to pursue education, training, and employment opportunities that align with their skills, experience, interests, and long-term goals. Immigration priorities are intended to respond to labour market needs at a particular point in time and should not be viewed as permanent. Choosing a program of study, occupation, or career path solely based on current immigration priorities may not improve future selection prospects, as provincial priorities will continue to evolve.&nbsp;</p><p>The Province will continue to publish updates when significant changes to selection priorities occur.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Expression of Interest Draws</strong>&nbsp;</h2><p>Nova Scotia conducts regular selections from its <a href="https://liveinnovascotia.com/eoi-process">Expression of Interest pool</a>. Applicants selected from the pool will be contacted directly by the Department.&nbsp;</p><p>For information on recent selections, visit the <a class="Hyperlink SCXW135197152 BCX8" href="https://liveinnovascotia.com/eoi-selection" target="_blank">Expression of Interest Draws page</a>.&nbsp;</p></div>

 <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p><em>April 27, 2026</em><br><br><strong>Nominee Program Priorities in Nova Scotia</strong>&nbsp;</p><p>Nova Scotia’s nominee programs are focused on addressing the province’s most critical labour‑market needs while managing limited nomination spaces, with an emphasis on candidates who demonstrate a genuine intention to live, work, and settle long‑term in the province.&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Current priority focus</strong>&nbsp;</h2><h3><strong>Healthcare and skilled trades occupations</strong></h3><p>These occupations are a top priority for nomination at TEER levels 0–4 only and may be considered for both:&nbsp;</p><ul><li>International applicants, and&nbsp;</li><li>Temporary residents already living and working in Nova Scotia&nbsp;</li></ul><p>TEER 5 occupations are not being prioritized.&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Other priority occupations</strong>&nbsp;</h3><p>In addition to healthcare and skilled trades, Nova Scotia may consider nominations for temporary residents who are already living and working in the province in the following occupational groups, where they align with program criteria and labour‑market needs:&nbsp;</p><ul><li>NOC 2 – Natural and Applied Sciences and Related Occupations&nbsp;</li><li>NOC 4 – Education, Law and Social, Community and Government Services&nbsp;</li><li>NOC 8 – Natural Resources, Agriculture and Related Production Occupations&nbsp;</li><li>NOC 9 – Manufacturing and Utilities&nbsp;</li></ul><p>Consideration under these occupational groups is limited to TEER levels 0–4 only.&nbsp;</p><p>TEER 5 occupations are not being prioritized at this time.&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Limited consideration for remaining occupations</strong>&nbsp;</h2><p>For occupations outside the priority groups, nominations are currently limited to:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><ul><li>TEER 0, 1, and 2 only, and&nbsp;</li><li>Temporary residents already living and working in Nova Scotia&nbsp;</li></ul><p>TEER 5 occupations are not being prioritized in any category at this time.&nbsp;</p><p>Program priorities may shift as labour‑market needs and nomination allocations change. Applicants are encouraged to monitor program updates regularly.&nbsp;</p></div>

 <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p><em>April 27, 2026</em>&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Effective May 1, 2026</strong>&nbsp;</h2><p>Effective May 1, 2026, Nova Scotia will introduce a 12‑month validity period for Expressions of Interest (EOIs) as part of improved inventory management and program efficiency measures. To support a smooth transition, a phased approach will apply to EOIs currently in the system.&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>Why this change is being introduced</strong>&nbsp;</h2><p>These measures ensure that EOIs in the system are current, accurate, and aligned with program and labour‑market needs, while maintaining a sufficient and stable inventory to support nominations and processing in 2026<strong>.</strong>&nbsp;</p><p>Applicants are encouraged to monitor program updates and submit a new EOI if their circumstances change or if their EOI expires.&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>How the transition will work</strong>&nbsp;</h2><h3><strong>EOIs submitted before May 1, 2024</strong>&nbsp;</h3><ul><li>EOIs submitted two or more years ago (before May 1, 2024) will be closed effective May 1, 2026.&nbsp;</li><li>Closing an EOI is not a refusal.&nbsp;</li><li>Affected candidates may submit a new EOI at any time, provided they meet eligibility requirements.&nbsp;</li></ul><h3><strong>EOIs submitted between May 1, 2024 and April 30, 2026</strong>&nbsp;</h3><ul><li>EOIs submitted during this period will remain active.&nbsp;</li><li>If not selected, these EOIs will expire on April 30, 2027.&nbsp;</li><li>No action is required from applicants at this time unless their circumstances change.&nbsp;</li></ul><h3><strong>EOIs submitted on or after May 1,  <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p><strong>November 28, 2025</strong></p><p>Nova Scotia is formalizing an expression of interest (EOI) process for all streams of the Nova Scotia Nominee Program (NSNP) and for Designations and Endorsements under the Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP). &nbsp;<br>&nbsp;<br>This important step will bring clarity and predictability to both applicants and employers, while ensuring the Province can respond to changing labour market needs and support sustainable, long-term community growth.&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>How does an EOI Process help Nova Scotia meet its labour needs?&nbsp;</strong>&nbsp;</h2><p>The number of individuals interested in immigrating to Nova Scotia is significantly higher than the number of applications the province can approve under its annual federal allocation from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>The EOI model is widely used across immigration systems in Canada. It enables Nova Scotia to identify and invite submissions that best align with the province’s evolving economic priorities, at the discretion of the department of Labour, Skills and Immigration (LSI). These priorities could include sectors such as healthcare, construction and trades, STEM, natural resources and manufacturing, along with broader community and workforce needs. Please continue to visit our website for published draw information.&nbsp;</p><p>The EOI model also sets clear expectations for the candidate and employer while providing a transparent and predictable experience.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><h2><strong>How our EOI process works: NSNP and AIP&nbsp;</strong>&nbsp;</h2><ul><li><strong>Submit your expression of interest:</strong>&nbsp;<br>Candidates (NSNP) and employers (AIP) will continue to submit full applications. These submissions are treated as EOIs and entered into an EOI pool.&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li><li><strong>Draws for processing:</strong>&nbsp;<br>Nova Scotia will conduct periodic draws to select EOIs for processing. Factors that may guide selection include provincial priorities, remaining allocation, EOI pool volume, and program integrity considerations.&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li><li><strong>If selected:</strong>&nbsp;<br>You will receive direct communication from our department confirming your submission has been chosen for processing. Being selected means your submission aligns with current priorities. It does <em>not</em> guarantee approval.&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</li><li><strong>If not selected:</strong>&nbsp;<br>Your submission remains in the EOI pool. If you do not receive communication, your status has not changed.&nbsp;</li></ul><h2><strong>Existing Submissions</strong>&nbsp;</h2><p>All submissions to Nova Scotia’s immigration programs — regardless of when they were submitted — are considered EOIs. If it is selected for processing, you will receive direct communications from our department. Until then, the status of your submission has not changed, and we have no further updates to provide.&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: When will my EOI be selected for processing?</strong>&nbsp;</h3><p><strong>A: </strong>We are unable to provide a timeline for the selection of EOIs for processing. We are unable to guarantee that your EOI will be selected for processing. You may wish to monitor our website for periodic draw information.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Does this announcement impact AIP Designations?&nbsp;</strong>&nbsp;</h3><p><strong>A:</strong> Yes, AIP designation submissions are considered an EOI and will follow the same process outlined above.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><h3><strong>Q: Am I still submitting a full application?</strong>&nbsp;</h3><p><strong>A:</strong> Yes. Priority areas may include sectors such as healthcare, construction and trades, STEM, natural resources, manufacturing, and other workforce needs across the province. These priorities will evolve over time based on labour market data and government direction. Updated selection information will be published on our website.&nbsp;</p></div>

 <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p><strong>July 1, 2024</strong><br><br>As<strong> </strong>of July 1, 2024,<strong> any NSNP 100 form on which the applicant’s contact information is replaced by a representative’s contact information will be denied at intake. The <a href="https://liveinnovascotia.com/sites/default/files/2024-05/NSNP-50-5Dec2014-English-Fillable.pdf">NSNP 50—Use of a Representative form</a> is used to capture the information of a representative, either paid or unpaid, who has permission to conduct business on behalf of the applicant.
