Residential Tenancies: guide to resolving disputes between tenants and landlords
 Processes available to tenants and landlords to resolve disputes.
 This guide outlines the options available to tenants and landlords to resolve a dispute when they aren’t able to resolve the dispute themselves.
 Information includes:
 tenant and landlord relationship
 resolving disputes on your own
 how to get help from the Residential Tenancies Program to resolve disputes
 how to make an Application to Director
 mediation and hearing process
 Order of the Director (decision)
 how to appeal the Order of the Director (decision)
