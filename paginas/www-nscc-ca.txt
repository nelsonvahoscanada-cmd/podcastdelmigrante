 Land Acknowledgement
 NSCC is located in Mi’kma’ki, the unceded territory and traditional homeland of the Mi’kmaq Nation. Our relationship is based on a series of peace and friendship treaties between the Mi’kmaq Nation and the Crown, dating back to 1725.
 As Treaty beneficiaries, we recognize that we are all Treaty People.

 In the news
 Tree for Boston cutting ceremony 2026 
 The 2026 Tree for Boston was cut down in East Amherst on Wednesday. NSCC NRET students helped prepare the tree for its journey to Boston, while faculty member Waddie Long cut the white spruce during a community ceremony.
 Nov. 18, 2026 Government of Nova Scotia 
 Town of Amherst renews support for NSCC students 
 The Town of Amherst is continuing its longstanding partnership with NSCC, committing another $45,000 over three years to help local students pursue post-secondary education. The Town's total financial commitment since 2018 reaches $203,000 and has supported 78 student awards through 2026, with another 21 planned over the next three years.
 Nov. 13, 2026 Town of Amherst 
 CPCI celebrates excellence in Canadian precast with 10 winners 
 The Canadian Precast Concrete Institute (CPCI) recognized 10 outstanding projects at its inaugural Project Awards Gala. The projects represent innovation, creativity and collaboration shaping Canada's built environment. The student housing at Ivany Campus was a recipient of the award, recognizing the project's innovative design, sustainability and precision through an integrated precast concrete approach.
