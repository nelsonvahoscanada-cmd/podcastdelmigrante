 Alberta Referendum – October 19, 2026 
 Three Orders in Council set a standalone referendum for ten questions for October 19, 2026.
 O.C. 160/2026 , approved May 28, 2026, set out Question 10 and ordered the results of the referendum on this question are not to be binding.
 O.C. 110/2026 , approved March 31, 2026, set out Questions 1 to 5 and ordered the results of the referendum on these questions are not to be binding.
 O.C. 109/2026 , approved March 31, 2026, set out constitutional Questions 6 to 9.
 Full Official Results are available at officialresults.elections.ab.ca .
