Customer eligibility verification: Your Energy Rebate Program
 Bulk vendors (residential energy suppliers) can check customers’ eligibility for the Your Energy Rebate Program.
 Use the Your Energy Rebate Eligibility Verification System to check your customers’ eligibility for a rebate.
 It’s your responsibility to confirm the eligibility of your customers.
 Before you start
 Make sure you have:
 username and password (you get these when you register as a bulk vendor )
 civic address of delivery location or name of property owner
 Start now 
 Options
 You can call the Your Energy Rebate Program bulk vendor line if you’re unable to check customer eligibility online.
