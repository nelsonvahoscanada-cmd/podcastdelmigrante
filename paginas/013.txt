Option 1 - Alberta should remain a province in Canada.
 Option 2 - The Government of Alberta should commence the legal process required under the Canadian Constitution to hold a binding provincial referendum on whether or not Alberta should separate from Canada.

Due to this, results for 14 locations where voting area votes could not be reliably separated from each other are reported at the voting place level, rather than the voting area level. This combined result is shown against the first of those voting areas and other voting areas in that location will show zero. Voter turnout for these voting areas should be read at the electoral division level.
