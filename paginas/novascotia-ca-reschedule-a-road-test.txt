Reschedule a Road Test
 If you booked your road test online, you can use this service to change its location, date, and/or time. You can reschedule your road test if you have an existing online booking for a road test for the following:
 Option 
 Description 
 Class 5 
 Cars, pickup trucks, sports utility vehicles (SUVs) and vans weighing 14,000 kg or less 
 Class 1
 Semi-trailers and tractor-trailer combinations
 Class 2 
 Buses with more than 24 passengers, excluding school buses 
 School Bus
 School buses or school-purpose buses
 Class 3 
 Large trucks weighing more than 14,000 kg (dump truck, cement truck, garbage truck) 
 Class 4
 Ambulances and small buses which have a seat capacity of less than 24 passengers
 Condition 15 with Class 3 
 Large trucks weighing more than 14,000 kg (dump truck, cement truck, garbage truck) with approval for a heavy trailer, valid for towing a trailer over 4,500 kg 
 Condition 15 with Class 5
 Cars, pick-up trucks, sports utility vehicles and vans with approval for a heavy trailer, valid for towing a trailer over 4,500 kg
 Balance motorcycle* 
 Balance motorcycle test (Class 6) is needed for the beginner's motorcycle licence 
 Advanced motorcycle*
 Advanced motorcycle test (Class 6) is needed for the full motorcycle licence or approval, known as an endorsement
 Interview 
 Required if the Registry of Motor Vehicles has required an interview to be completed 
 *Motorcycle road tests are typically available between April and November, depending on weather and road conditions. 
 For all other bookings, please call us at one of the numbers listed below.
 If you do not have an existing booking, you can book a road test online .
 Required information
 To change your booking online, make sure you have your Nova Scotia driver's licence number.
 Reschedule or cancel free up to 48 hours before your appointment
 If you reschedule or cancel your booking with less than 48 hours' notice before your appointment, you will need to pay again when you rebook your road test.
 For questions or to book a road test over the phone, call us at:
 Local: 902-424-5851
 Toll-free: 1-800-898-7668
 Toll-free TTY: 1-877-404-0867
 Monday to Fridays between 8:30AM to 4:30PM, excluding holidays
