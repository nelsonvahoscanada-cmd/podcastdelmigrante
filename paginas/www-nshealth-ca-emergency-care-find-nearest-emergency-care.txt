Find Nearest Emergency Care
 Step 1 of 2 
 This tool will identify the closest open emergency facility capable of responding to your situation. Answering these questions will take less than a minute. 
 If you are experiencing a medical emergency, please call 9-1-1 for immediate assistance.

 Privacy
 This website won’t ask you to provide your name or any other personally identifiable information.
 If you would prefer to not answer questions online, you may call 8-1-1 who can provide assistance.
