Pay for Your Road Test Receipt (driving test)
 You need to pay for a road test (driving test) before you can schedule and take the test. You can book a Class 5 road test online, and pay with a credit card when you're booking. 
 Booking and paying for a Class 5 road test 
 You can book a Class 5 road test online , and pay with a credit card when you're booking. 
 If you would like to pay with debit card, credit card, cheque, money order, or cash, you must go to an Access Nova Scotia location to pay, and keep your receipt for booking online. 
 To book and pay for any road test other than a Class 5 
 You need a road test receipt to schedule the test. You also need to take the road test receipt to your road test at Registry of Motor Vehicles (you can print the receipt or have it available on your phone). If you don't have your receipt with you when you arrive at Registry of Motor Vehicles, it may delay your road test. 
 To book your test after paying, you then need to call the Registry of Motor Vehicles to schedule the test, and you need the receipt number as proof that you paid for the test. 
 Eligibility
 You can use this service to pay for a road test for a:
 class 1 (semi-trailer and tractor-trailer combinations) driver's licence
 class 2 (bus for over 24 passengers) driver's licence
 class 3 (vehicle or vehicle-trailer combinations over 14,000 kg) driver's licence
 class 4 (bus for 24 or fewer passengers) driver's licence
 class 5 (passenger vehicle) driver's licence
 class 6 (motorcycle) driver's licence
 motorcycle endorsement (advanced skills road test, not a balance test)
 school bus endorsement
 heavy trailer endorsement (select the vehicle that you're going to drive, for example, class 5 or class 3)
 You can pay for the road test for yourself or someone else.
 How to pay
 Complete the payment form online.
 Include payment with your form.
 Submit your completed form and payment.
 The service emails you a road test receipt. Keep a copy for your records.
 How long it takes
 Your road test payment is received as soon as you submit your completed form and payment.
 Cost
 Activity
 Fee
 Road test
 $52.54
 Payment options
 Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), debit card, cheque, money order, cash. Make your cheque or money order payable to the Minister of Finance. Payment options may vary depending on whether you pay online or in person.
 Before you start
 Make sure you:
 know the type of road test you want to take
 have your driver's licence number (you can find this on the front of your driver's licence)
 Pay online
 When you pay online you need to pay the fee by credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Visa Debit, Debit Mastercard). You can't access the form online if you're outside Canada or your computer's IP address is outside Canada.
