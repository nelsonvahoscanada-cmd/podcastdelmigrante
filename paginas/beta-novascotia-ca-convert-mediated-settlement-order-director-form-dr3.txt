Convert mediated settlement into Order of the Director (Form DR3)
 Landlords and tenants use Form DR3 to request a mediated settlement is converted into an Order of the Director (decision) when the other party hasn’t met (complied with) the terms of the mediated settlement.
 Use the Form DR3: Convert Mediated Settlement into Order of the Director if you believe the other party has failed to meet (comply with) the terms of a mediated settlement and you wish to convert the mediated settlement into an Order of the Director (decision).
 Who can use this form
 Landlords and tenants need to use this form to request that a mediated settlement is converted into an Order of the Director (decision).
 What you need to do
 Complete the form.
 Send a copy of the form to the other party.
 Send your completed form to the residential tenancy officer that issued the mediated settlement.
 How long it takes
 It should take 1 week to receive a decision (Order of the Director). It can take longer if more information is needed or if your application hasn’t been filled in correctly.
 Cost
 There is no cost to submit the form.
 Before you start
 Make sure you have:
 file number and date of mediated settlement
 description of the other party’s non-compliance with the mediated settlement
 supporting documentation
