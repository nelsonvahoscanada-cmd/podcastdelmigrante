Residential tenancy guides and factsheets
 Residential tenancy guides and factsheets provide tenants and landlords with information about their individual rights and responsibilities.
 Residential tenancy guides and factsheets provide information on the rights and responsibilities of tenants and landlords. They also describe processes to be used for dispute resolution, land-lease communities (mobile parks) and co-operative housing.
 The guides and factsheets address the most common situations that landlords and tenants deal with.
