Capital Costs and Pro-forma Operating Budget Template: Affordable Housing Development Program
 If you’re applying for funding through the Affordable Housing Development Program, you need to complete the Capital Costs and Pro-forma Operating Budget Template to provide a detailed cost estimate of your project.
 Use the Capital Costs and Pro-forma Operating Budget Template Form to provide a detailed cost estimate of your project. You need to submit the template as part of the Affordable Housing Development Program application process .
 The budget template outlines your eligible and ineligible project costs, including:
 land costs
 building costs
 development costs
 pro forma operating budget
 Who can use this form
 You need to use this template if you’re applying for the Affordable Housing Development Program.
