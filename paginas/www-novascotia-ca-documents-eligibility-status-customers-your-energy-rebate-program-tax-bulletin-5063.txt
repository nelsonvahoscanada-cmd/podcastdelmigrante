Eligibility status of customers for Your Energy Rebate Program: tax bulletin 5063
 How residential energy suppliers registered under the Your Energy Rebate Program can check customer eligibility using an eligibility tool.
 This tax information bulletin provides information on using an eligibility tool to check customer eligibility for the point of sale rebate.
 Information includes:
 where to access the eligibility tool
 how to use the eligibility tool
 mobile homes located in mobile home parks
 mobile homes not located in mobile home parks
