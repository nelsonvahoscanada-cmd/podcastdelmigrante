Annual production statistics report: Aquaculture Licence
 Aquaculture Licence holders need to submit an annual production statistics report. The report outlines your aquaculture production and employment statistics in a calendar year.
 Use the Annual Production Statistics Report Form to report your aquaculture production and employment statistics.
 Email address change
 You need to notify the Department of Fisheries and Aquaculture if there are any changes to your email address (you need to update your email with the department before you submit your report).
 Annual statistics notice
 You need to report your production statistics each year. You should get an annual statistics notice by email (including your passcode and deadline for submitting your report). It’s your responsibility to report on time.
 Who can use this form
 Aquaculture Licence holders need to use this form.
 The report is due each year. You need to complete the report even if you didn’t conduct aquaculture at the licensed site.
 You need to report separately for each Aquaculture Licence (each licence needs a separate report).
 What you need to do
 Complete the report online.
 The service emails you a confirmation. Keep a copy for your records.
 How long it takes
 It should take 3 to 5 days for the Department of Fisheries and Aquaculture to update their records.
 Cost
 There is no cost to submit the report.
 Before you start
 Make sure you:
 have your passcode (you can find this on your annual statistics notice)
 have your Aquaculture Licence number
 have your production numbers for the reporting year
 are an authorized contact for the Aquaculture Licence
 Online
 When you complete the report online, you need an email address (this is where your confirmation is sent). You also need to have a desktop computer or laptop (you can’t complete the report on a phone, tablet or other device).
