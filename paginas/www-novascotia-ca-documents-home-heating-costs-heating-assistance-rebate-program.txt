Home heating costs: Heating Assistance Rebate Program
 Overview of home heating costs and heating bill requirements for the Heating Assistance Rebate Program.
 This document outlines eligibility for home heating costs and heating bill requirements, including what information receipts need to include.
 Information includes:
 proof of heat
 electric heat
 furnace oil, propane, natural gas
 retail Purchases (like wood pellets, propane, kerosene)
 wood
 ineligible heating costs
