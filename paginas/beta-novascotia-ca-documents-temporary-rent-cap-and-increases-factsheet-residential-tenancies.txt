Temporary rent cap and increases factsheet: Residential Tenancies
 Overview of the rent cap and how much a landlord can increase rent each year for a residential rental unit.
 This document outlines the rent cap and rent increases on residential rental units for tenants and landlords.
 Information includes:
 what is the rent cap
 what the rent cap means for tenants
 what the rent cap means for landlords
 what to do if you disagree with a rent increase
