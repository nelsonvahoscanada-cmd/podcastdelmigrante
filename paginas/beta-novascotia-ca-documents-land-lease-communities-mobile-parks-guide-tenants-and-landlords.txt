Land-lease communities (mobile parks): guide for tenants and landlords
 Rights and responsibilities of tenants and landlords of land-lease communities (mobile parks).
 This guide outlines the rights and responsibilities of landlords and tenants where tenants rent space for their manufactured home (mobile home) from a landlord in a land-lease community (mobile park).
 Information includes:
 who is responsible for what
 leases
 security (damage) deposits
 how a tenant can end a tenancy (lease)
 how a landlord can end a tenancy (lease)
 regular rental increases
 rental increases greater than the AARIA
 eligible expenses for consideration in rent increases greater than the AARIA
 buying and selling manufactured homes (mobile homes)
 resolving disputes
