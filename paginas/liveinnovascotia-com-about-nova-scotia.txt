About Nova Scotia 
 Nova Scotia offers a quality of life that is disappearing in much of North America, allowing you to keep everything in perspective: work, family, relaxation, and responsibility.
 We are fortunate to be surrounded by the natural beauty of the rolling sea, lush green forests, lakes, and farmland. All of this, combined with our growing cities and low cost of living, makes Nova Scotia one of the most enjoyable places to live in Canada.
 There are many reasons Nova Scotia will feel like home in no time. Here are a few:
 ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITIES
 Nova Scotia's economy is growing fast and we're topping many of the 'best of' lists. With opportunity in almost every sector, Nova Scotia needs your talent.
 WELCOMING COMMUNITIES
 With a population of over 1 million, community is very important in Nova Scotia. You and your family will feel wanted and supported by Nova Scotians and newcomers alike with our East Coast charm. 
 CANADA'S OCEAN PLAYGROUND
 Surrounded by nature with easy access to active recreation in your community and on the coast.
If you want to experience Nova Scotia, and decide if it's right for you, start planning your trip here .
 CHOICE OF COMMUNITIES
 Your choice of cities, towns and true rural to live the way you’ve been longing for with the right pace of life for you.
 Nova Scotia's History and Culture 
 One of the first established areas in Canada, Nova Scotia has a diverse history of Indigenous peoples - the Mi'kmaq, Gaelic, Acadian, and African Nova Scotian cultures that dates back hundreds of years and, in the case of the Mi’kmaq, thousands of years.
 Nova Scotia is home to more than 100 cultures and ethnicities from all over the globe.
 Welcoming Diversity 
 Our history and diversity are reflected in the many museums, festivals and events held in cities, towns, and communities throughout the province. It is also expressed in our music, art, food, and traditions.
 Beautiful coastline within 56km, no matter where you are in Nova Scotia. 
 Nova Scotia is home to 5 UNESCO World Heritage Sites. 
 Hundreds of cultural events and festivals are hosted each year. 
 Century old stories come to life through 26 Canadian National Historic Sites. 
 Find the Community for You 
 Have your choice of cities, towns and true rural to live the way you've been longing for. Nova Scotia offers communities that fit any lifestyle.
