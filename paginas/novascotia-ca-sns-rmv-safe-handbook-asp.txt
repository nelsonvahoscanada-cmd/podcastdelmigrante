Driver's Handbook
 Driving rules (traffic and safe driving) that you need to follow under the Traffic Safety Act and information about licensing, registration, inspection and accident reporting.
 This handbook explains driving rules and requirements under the Traffic Safety Act and regulations. It also includes advice to help you be a safer driver and avoid being in an accident.
 Information includes:
 driver’s licence classes and graduated licensing
 driving rules (traffic and safe driving)
 road signs, pavement markings and work zones
 vehicle safety, passenger safety and safe driving practices
 poor driving conditions and emergencies
 impaired driving
 vehicle registration and insurance
 motorcycles
