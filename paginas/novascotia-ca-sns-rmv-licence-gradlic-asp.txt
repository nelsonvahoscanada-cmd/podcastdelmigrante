 Graduated Driver Licensing Program
 Under the Graduated Driver Licensing (GDL) program, any new driver, regardless of age, who applies for a learner’s licence will operate under the GDL program.
 The GDL program consists of three separate stages. In each stage, the new driver is subject to driving restrictions and conditions. To graduate to the next phase, the driver must successfully complete a series of milestones. This allows a new driver to gain experience and build skills gradually over time, rather than being exposed to the full range of driving challenges and situations all at once.
 The three stages of the GDL program are:
 learner’s licence
 newly licensed driver
 restricted individual
 Once a new driver has successfully completed all three stages, all the restrictions associated with the GDL program are removed and the driver is considered a fully experienced driver. 
 Passenger Vehicle Drivers
 Learner’s Licence Stage
 Operating conditions at this stage include:
 no passengers, except a supervising driver who is not enrolled in the GDL program
 zero alcohol or drugs in the learning driver’s system
 To progress to the next stage, the driver must complete the minimum practice period and successfully pass a road test. 
 The minimum practice period is 12 months. The practice period can be reduced to a minimum of 9 months if the driver completes an approved driver education course with at least 25 hours of online or classroom learning and at least 10 hours of learning in a vehicle. Suspensions will delay graduation to the newly licensed driver stage by a period of time equal to the minimum practice period.
 Once a road test has been successfully completed, the learner becomes a newly licensed driver for a minimum of two years.
 Newly Licensed Driver Stage
 Operating conditions include:
 zero alcohol or drugs in the newly licensed driver’s system
 only one front seat passenger and rear seat passengers limited to the number of available seat belts
 no upgrade beyond a class 5 driver's licence
 no driving between midnight and 5:00am, unless exempted from the nightly curfew or the newly licensed driver is accompanied by a supervising driver
 A newly licensed driver may apply for an exemption from the nighttime driving curfew to drive to and from work, go to school or volunteer as a firefighter or ground search and rescue. The driver must take the most direct route and is not permitted to have any passengers in the vehicle. An Application for Exemption from Nighttime Driving Curfew form must be properly completed, signed and submitted to the Registry of Motor Vehicles office with the applicable fee. 
 To graduate from the newly licensed driver stage, the driver must successfully complete an approved driver education course. You can take either the short course (6 hours of online or classroom learning) or the long course (at least 25 hours of online or classroom learning and at least 10 hours of learning in a vehicle). A copy of the graduation certificate must be provided, in person or by mail, to any Registry of Motor Vehicles office for recording purposes. If a graduation certificate is not provided to the Registry of Motor Vehicles, the driver will remain in the newly licensed stage indefinitely. 
 Licence suspensions will delay graduation to the restricted individual stage by 2 years from the date the licence is reinstated.
 A demerit point system will remain in effect for newly licensed drivers. These drivers will be subject to driver improvement action much earlier than is the case for experienced drivers.
 Restricted Individual Stage
 While in the restricted individual stage, the driver is subject to a special driving restricted called “condition 47”.
 Operating conditions include:
 zero alcohol or drugs in the restricted individual’s system
 the restricted individual cannot be a supervising driver for another driver
 The driver will remain in the restricted individual stage for 2 years. At the end of the two-year driving period, all restrictions associated with the GDL program are removed and the driver is considered a fully experienced driver. 
 Motorcycle Drivers
 Motorcycle Learner’s Licence Stage
 To obtain a motorcycle learner's licence, the new driver must:
 obtain a learner's (Class 7) driver's licence or Class 8 and be at least 16 years old
 successfully complete a motorcycle rules test
 successfully complete a practical skills test
 Operating conditions include:
 no passengers
 driving only during daylight hours (one half hour before sunrise to one half hour after sunset)
 zero alcohol or drugs in the learning driver’s system
 To progress to the next phase the driver must complete the minimum practice period and successfully pass a practical skills test. 
 The minimum practice periods are 3 months if an approved motorcycle course is taken, 6 months if not. Suspensions will delay graduation to the newly licensed driver stage by a period of time equal to the minimum practice period.
 Newly Licensed Motorcycle Driver Stage
 Operating conditions include:
 zero alcohol or drugs in the newly licensed driver’s system
 no upgrade beyond a Class 5 driver's licence
 no driving between midnight and 5:00am, unless exempted from the nightly curfew
 A newly licensed driver may apply for an exemption from the nighttime driving curfew to drive to or from work, go to school or volunteer as a firefighter or ground search and rescue. The driver must take the most direct route. An Application for Exemption from Nighttime Driving Curfew form must be properly completed, signed and submitted to a Registry of Motor Vehicles office with the applicable fee. 
 To graduate from the newly licensed driver stage, the driver must successfully complete a recognized motorcycle training program or an approved driver education course. A copy of the graduation certificate must be provided, in person or by mail, to any Registry of Motor Vehicles office for recording purposes. If a graduation certificate is not provided to the Registry of Motor Vehicles, the driver will remain in the newly licensed phase indefinitely. 
 Licence suspensions will delay graduation to the restricted individual stage by 2 years from the date the licence is reinstated.
 A demerit point system will remain in effect for newly licensed drivers. These drivers will be subject to driver improvement action much earlier than is the case for experienced drivers.
 Restricted Individual Stage
 While in the restricted individual stage, the motorcycle driver is subject to a special driving restricted called “condition 47”.
 Operating conditions include:
 zero alcohol or drugs in the restricted individual’s system
 the restricted individual cannot be a supervising driver for another driver
 The driver will remain in the restricted individual stage for 2 years. At the end of the two-year driving period, all restrictions associated with the GDL program are removed and the driver is considered a fully experienced driver. 
 Upgrade to a Motorcycle Driver's Licence for Regular Class Licence Holders
 Motorcycle licence endorsement:
 For regular class licence holders who are not under the GDL program, the driver with a valid licence (Class 1-5) may obtain a motorcycle learner's licence by successfully completing a rules test and practical skills test. Upon successful completion of the required tests, a paper learner motorcycle licence is issued which must be carried with your photo licence. 
 Operating conditions are the same as they are for a motorcycle learner licence (Initial licence). 
 Upgrade to motorcycle endorsement on driver's licence:
 the driver may upgrade to a motorcycle endorsement on a valid driver's licence (Class 1-5) after 3 months and successfully completing an advanced skills test, or 1 month if an approved motorcycle course has been successfully completed
