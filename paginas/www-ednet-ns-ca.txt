Nova Scotia Education Network
 The Nova Scotia Education Network (EDnet) is the Government of Nova Scotia's online public school and child care information hub. EDnet has important information for students, families and people who work in public schools and licensed child care centres. 
 More information about Nova Scotia's Department of Education and Early Childhood Development can be found on the Government of Nova Scotia website .
