Certificate of Service: Residential Tenancies (Form L1) 
 Landlords and tenants use Form L1 to certify that they’ve served residential tenancy documents on the other party.
 Use Form L1: Certificate of Service to certify that you’ve served residential tenancy documents on your landlord or tenant.
 Who can use this form
 Landlords and tenants use the form to certify that they’ve served residential tenancy documents on the other party.
 What you need to do
 Complete the form.
 Check the form for details on all required supporting documents.
 Take your completed form and supporting documents to Access Nova Scotia.
 How long it takes
 The Certificate of Service is completed once you take your completed form to Access Nova Scotia. It can take longer if more information is needed or if your form hasn’t been filled in correctly.
 Cost
 There is no cost to submit the form.
 Before you start
 Make sure you have:
 copies of the documents you served
 receipt showing proof that you prepaid and properly addressed the document if you served (delivered) it by registered mail, express post or courier service
 copy of the email showing the date and time you sent the document if you served (delivered) it by email
