 Features
 Become a Waste Sorting Champion Every properly sorted item helps to reduce waste, increase recycling, and keep our community cleaner and greener. Households that successfully demonstrate excellent waste sorting habits will receive a green cart featuring a giant GOLD STAR and earn the title of Waste Sorting Champion! 
 Online Permits Our new permit system is available for all municipal permits including; building, development, sewer, driveway, frontage culvert, water, solar, and demolition permits along with civic numbers and sign requests. 
 Active Transportation Share your thoughts as we develop an Active Transportation Strategy for Colchester, and help shape the future of walking and rolling in our communities! 
 Tourism Marketing Levy Learn more about the Tourism Marketing Levy, which mandates all accommodation providers in the region to collect and remit a 3% levy on all accommodations sold to overnight visitors within Truro, Stewiacke, Millbrook, and Colchester County. 
 Work With Us! Interested in a career with the Municipality of Colchester? Browse our current job opportunities and apply for job vacancies here. 
 Who Maintains My Road? Explore our road mapping application to learn who maintains your road. Search by civic number, Parcel Identifier (PID Number), or street name. 
 Colchester County-Wide Planning The new Colchester Municipal Planning Strategy and Land Use By-law are now in effect county wide. Discover how these changes shape your community’s future by exploring the updated policies, reviewing the zoning map, and consulting the By-law. 
 Parks & Trails Parks and trails in Colchester County are wonderful to explore year-round. Plan your adventure here! 
 Stay Informed Read the latest edition of our quarterly newsletter to stay up-to-date on municipal news, programs, and services. 
 Current Planning Applications View current planning applications for rezoning, by-law amendments and development agreements within Colchester County. 
 Fundy Discovery Site Fundy Discovery Site is one of the most convenient and accessible places to experience the incredible tidal phenomena of Canada’s Bay of Fundy – home to the highest tides on Earth! 
 Cozy Colchester Interested in upgrading your home with heat pumps, insulation, or other energy-efficiency projects? Cozy Colchester is here to help with low-interest financing! Save money, boost comfort, and reduce emissions.
