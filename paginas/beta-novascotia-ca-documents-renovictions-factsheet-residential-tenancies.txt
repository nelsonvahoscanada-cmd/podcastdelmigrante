Renovictions factsheet: Residential Tenancies
 Overview of what tenants and landlords need to know about renovictions (ending a tenancy for renovations).
 This document outlines the process for ending a tenancy because of renovations for tenants and landlords.
 Information includes:
 what is a renoviction
 what happens if the tenant and landlord agree
 what happens if the tenant changes their mind
 steps if the tenant and landlord don't agree
 how a decision is made about the renoviction
 additional compensation
 resources
