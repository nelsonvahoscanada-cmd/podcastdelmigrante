Request eligibility verification for tuition waivers and supports for former children in care
 Eligibility verification confirms that you’re eligible for resources, supports and programs available to former children in care. This includes tuition waivers.
 You can request eligibility verification for tuition waivers and supports for former children in care.
 Tuition waiver
 The Tuition Waiver Program helps make post-secondary education more accessible to former children in care or youth who received youth services through the Department of Community Services between the ages of 16 and 18 under the Children and Family Services Act. Some post-secondary institutions waive tuition and mandatory program fees with proof of eligibility as former children in care.
 Children’s Aid Foundation of Nova Scotia
 The Children’s Aid Foundation of Nova Scotia provides programs and resources for former children in care. Eligibility varies depending on the grant funding criteria. Some grant funding requires proof of eligibility for former children in care to apply.
 Other resources, supports and programs
 Agencies and organizations sometimes require proof of eligibility for former children in care to apply for resources, supports and programs.
 Who can use this form
 You can request eligibility verification if:
 you’re a Former Child in Permanent Care
 you received youth services through the Department of Community Services between the ages of 16 and 18 under the Children and Family Services Act
 you’re adopted and were a former child in permanent care in Nova Scotia
 What you need to do
 Complete the form online.
 Check the form for details on all required supporting documents.
 Submit your completed form and supporting documents.
 How long it takes
 It should take 1 to 2 weeks for the Disclosure Program to receive your eligibility verification letter. It can take longer if more information is needed or if your form hasn’t been filled in correctly.
 Cost
 There is no cost to request eligibility verification.
 Before you start
 Make sure you have 1 piece of government-issued identification (like a driver’s licence, Secure Certificate of Indian Status, passport or NEXUS card).
 Online
 When you request eligibility verification online you need to upload any supporting documents in DOCX, PDF or JPEG format.
 Start now 
 Other ways to submit the form
 You can use the Request for Eligibility Verification Form (PDF 95 kB) if you're unable to request eligibility verification online. Send your completed form and supporting documents by mail or email to the Disclosure Program.
