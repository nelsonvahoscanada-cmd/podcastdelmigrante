Telephone hearing preparation factsheet: Residential Tenancies
 Overview of how to get ready for a telephone hearing with the Residential Tenancies Program.
 This document outlines what you need to apply for a telephone hearing and how to prepare.
 Information includes:
 when the Residential Tenancies Program can help
 applying for a hearing
 giving documents to the other person (“serving” them)
 gathering and submitting your evidence
 resources
 checklist
