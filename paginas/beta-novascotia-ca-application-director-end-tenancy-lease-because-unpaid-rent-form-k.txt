Application to Director to end tenancy (lease) because of unpaid rent (Form K)
 Apply to the Director of Residential Tenancies to end a tenant’s lease because of unpaid rent without participating in a hearing or mediation with the tenant.
 Use Form K: Application to Director – Rental Arrears to end a tenant’s lease because of unpaid rent without participating in a hearing or mediation with the tenant.
 Eligibility
 You can only use Form K if you already served your tenant with Form D: Notice to Quit for Rental Arrears . You can submit Form K 10 days or more after you serve Form D.
 You can’t use Form K if the tenant:
 pays the rent within 10 days of receiving Form D
 has applied to the director with Form J to have Form D set aside
 How to apply
 Complete the application form.
 Sign page F5 of the application in the presence of a lawyer or commissioner of oaths.
 Take your completed application, a copy of Form D you served on your tenant and payment to Access Nova Scotia.
 Access Nova Scotia gives you a copy of Form K for you to serve on your tenant.
 You or your representative need to serve Form K to the same tenant you served Form D to.
 Complete Form L1: Certificate of Service after your tenant has been served.
 Within 7 days of applying, drop off your completed Form L1: Certificate of Service at Access Nova Scotia.
 Access Nova Scotia processes Form K and assigns it a residential tenancy officer.
 The residential tenancy officer reviews Form K and renders a decision (Order of the Director). The decision (Order of the Director) is mailed to the applicant and tenant.
 How long it takes
 It should take 2 weeks to receive a decision (Order of the Director). It can take longer if more information is needed or if your application hasn’t been filled in correctly.
 Cost
 Activity
 Fee
 Registration
 $31.15
 Payment options
 Visa, MasterCard, American Express, debit card, cheque, money order, cash. A cheque or money order should be made payable to the Minister of Finance. Payment options may vary depending on how you apply.
 Before you start
 Make sure you have:
 tenant’s name, address, phone number and email address
 date Form D was served on the tenant
 date to end the tenancy
 copy of Form D
 term of lease
 amount of rent due each week or month
 amount of rent paid since Form D was served
 date tenant moved in and out
 amount of security deposit and date paid
