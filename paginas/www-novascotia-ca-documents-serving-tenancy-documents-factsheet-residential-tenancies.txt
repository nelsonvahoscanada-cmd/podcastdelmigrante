Serving tenancy documents factsheet: Residential Tenancies
 Overview of what tenants and landlords need to know about serving residential tenancy documents and notices.
 This document outlines the process for serving (delivering) residential tenancy documents and notices on your landlord or tenant.
 Information includes:
 what "service" is and why it's important
 why you need to serve residential tenancy documents and notices
 timing and deadlines
 Certificate of Service
 how you can serve documents and notices
 resources
