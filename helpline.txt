 When you need someone to talk to, we're here to help. 
 The help line is toll-free, anonymous, confidential and staffed by trained volunteers. 
 Who calls Kamatsiaqut Helpline? 
 Family members
 Friends, co-workers and community members
 Spouses, boyfriends/girlfriends and people in complicated relationships
 People concerned about the well being of someone they know
 Why do they call the Helpline? 
 Someone is suicidal or in emotional distress
 People who are feeling sad, alone, overwhelmed, depressed, or anxious
 Struggling with a situation or person at home, work, or school
 Concerned about drugs, alcohol and other substance abuse
 People call for many different reasons. Whatever is affecting your day, your life, we are here to provide you with support. We have helped many people, just like you. If you're struggling, please call.
