 Consumer Price Index - September 2026 
 Information released today by Statistics Canada indicates the Yellowknife All-Items Consumer Price Index in September rose by 4.7% compared to one year ago. By comparison, annual prices increased by 3.1% for Canada, 3.5% for Edmonton, 6.1% for Whitehorse, and 3.0% for Iqaluit.
