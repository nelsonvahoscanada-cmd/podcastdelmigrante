IMMIGRATION
Settling and Succeeding in Nunavut
Settling in a new territory is a major challenge, both personally and professionally. Carrefour Nunavut supports newcomers at every stage of their journey to promote sustainable, successful, and inclusive integration.

Our approach aims to support every stage of the settlement process while fostering autonomy and professional success.


Personalized support

Our goal is simple — to provide you with the tools, resources, and guidance you need to successfully settle in Nunavut, both socially and economically.

Access to information

We support you throughout your settlement process by offering:
- Personalized guidance at every stage of your integration
- Practical information about life in Nunavut (employment, housing, services, etc.
- Assistance with administrative procedures

Support for employers

We also assist employers in their integration efforts by providing:
- Awareness on hiring bilingual immigrant talent
- Support for recruitment and workplace integration
- Connections between employers and qualified candidates

Needs assessment and orientation

We offer an individualized approach to better understand your situation and guide you effectively:
- A full assessment of your needs and the development of a personalized settlement plan
- A tailored analysis of your profile and goals
- Referrals to employment, education, and health services
- Language assessment and access to appropriate training

Building connections and cultural integration

Becauseintegration also depends on social connection, we encourage your participationin community life through:
- Activities that promote inclusion and civic engagement
- Conversation circles (French and English)·       Matching with established community members
- Social and cultural activities
- Referrals to other local organizations and services

Successful integration, a sustainable future

Through a human centered, structured approach adapted to the realities of the territory, Carrefour Nunavut actively helps build strong and promising integration pathways.

Are you a newcomer or an employer? Contact Carrefour Nunavut to benefit from personalized support.
