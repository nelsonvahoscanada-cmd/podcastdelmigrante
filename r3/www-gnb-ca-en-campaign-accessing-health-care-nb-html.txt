Accessing health care in New Brunswick
 Find the right care for your situation.
 ! 
 In case of an emergency 
 If you are having difficulty breathing, chest pain or other severe symptoms, call 911 immediately or go to your local emergency room. Other health concerns would be considered non-emergency.
 If you are experiencing addiction or mental health concerns, contact:
 the free provincial addiction and mental health helpline: 1-866-355-5550 
 Canada Suicide Prevention Service: call or text 988 or visit 988.ca 
 Kids Help Phone: 1-800-668-6868 
 On this page
 If you already have a primary care provider (family doctor or nurse practitioner), start there whenever you can. If you don’t have a primary care provider, make sure to register with NB Health Link . 
 Know your options for care
 Find non-emergency care that works for you, in person or virtually, with or without a primary care provider (family doctor or nurse practitioner). 
 Start here: check your symptoms 
 Use the symptom checker to find out how serious your symptoms are, if a healthcare visit is needed and where to get help (self-care, pharmacist, clinic, virtual care, call 811 or go to the emergency room). 
 Tele-Care 811 
 Available: 24/7 
 How to access
 Call 811 
 About
 Get 24-hour bilingual support from a nurse who will help you assess your situation, offer non-emergency health advice and access services in your community.
 Medical (walk-in) clinics 
 Available: Varies by location 
 How to access
 Call your local clinic.
 About
 In-person health care consultations for a common illness, injury, infection or general health concerns. 
 Virtual Care NB 
 Available: 8 a.m.–8 p.m., 7 days 
 How to access
 Visit the page or call 811 .
 About
 Virtual Care NB can provide virtual consultations for common illnesses, injuries, infections or general health concerns. 
 Pharmacy services 
 Available: Varies by pharmacy 
 How to access
 Contact your local pharmacy as most will require an appointment.
 About
 Publicly funded health-care services for common ailments offered by New Brunswick pharmacists, includes prescription renewal, uncomplicated urinary tract infections, publicly funded vaccines and more.
 NB Health Link 
 Available: Mon–Fri, 9 a.m.–4 p.m. 
 How to access
 Register by visiting the website or calling 1-833-354-2300 .
 About
 NB Health Link is the provincial wait list for New Brunswickers without a family doctor or nurse practitioner. It also offers people registered with the program access to a team of health care professionals who can care for them until they can secure a permanent provider. 
 Mental health and addictions
 Find support for mental health, addiction and substance use concerns
 Addiction and Mental Health Helpline
 Call the New Brunswick Addiction and Mental Health Helpline at 1-866-355-5550 for free, 24-hour support. 
 Addictions and substance use
 Find services to help if you are struggling with substance use or have a gambling issue.
 Mental health
 Find mental health support for vulnerable communities, whatever your stage of life.
 Manage your health
 Self-serve tools to help you manage your health.
 MyHealthNB 
 Available: 24/7 
 How to access
 Download the app or visit the website to register. Open 24 hours a day, seven days a week on your personal computer or smartphone.
 About
 View your health information such as results of blood and urine tests, ultrasounds and others. You can also consult the list of recent medications obtained in pharmacies, access your immunization records, and access related services in one secure place.
 Hospitals
 Vitalité and Horizon health networks are the two authorities responsible for managing and delivering health services in the province.
 Horizon
 Find facilities and services across New Brunswick within the Horizon Health Network. 
 Vitalité
 View the Vitalité Health Network service directory and find a point of service.
