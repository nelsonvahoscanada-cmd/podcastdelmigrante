Health system and services
 Primary health care, 811, regional health authorities, NB Health Link , clinics, sexual health, pharmacies and more. 
 On this page
 Emergency and crisis care
 911
 911 is the emergency telephone number to call if there is a threat to health, safety or property.
 Ambulance services
 Information about ambulances and related fees.
 Emergency departments - Horizon hospitals
 Emergency department locations, hours of operation and triage information for hospitals in the Horizon Health Network.
 Emergency departments - Vitalité hospitals
 Emergency department locations, hours of operation and triage information for hospitals in the Vitalité Health Network.
 Non-urgent health care
 Primary care providers
 If you already have a family doctor or nurse practitioner, contact them for non-urgent health care.
 Collaborative team-based primary health care
 Team-based primary health care connects patients who don't already have a primary health-care provider with a group of providers who share responsibility for meeting their ongoing health needs.
 NB Health Link
 NB Health Link is a program for New Brunswickers without access to a primary health-care provider.
 Get information 24 hours a day
 211 New Brunswick
 Find community, government and social services.
 Check your symptoms
 Use this symptom checker to learn how serious your symptoms are, if a health care visit is needed and what steps you can take to relieve your symptoms at home.
 Digital Health Strategy
 A vision for a modern, connected healthcare system with digital solutions that puts people at the center of care.
 Patient safety surveillance
 A strong health system learns from patient safety problems, including mistakes and near misses, to improve care and prevent future issues.
 Tele-Care 811
 Get 24-hour support from a nurse, determine if you need to seek care or get referred for an appointment. 
 Your rights as a patient
 Information for patients and families about health care rights, responsibilities of care, and how to give feedback.
 Health care options
 Addictions and substance use
 Find services to help if you are struggling with a substance (i.e. alcohol and/or drugs) or have a gambling issue.
 Extra-Mural Program
 Home and community-based health care for people of all ages across New Brunswick.
 Pharmacy services
 Health-care services offered by New Brunswick pharmacists and funded by the Department of Health.
 Medical (walk-in) clinics
 In-person health-care consultations for a common illness, injury, infection or general health concerns. 
 Mental health
 Find mental health support for vulnerable communities, whatever your stage of life.
 Public health clinics
 Find public health clinics across the province.
 Regional Health Authorities
 Vitalité and Horizon Network are the two authorities responsible for managing and delivering health services in the province.
 Sexual health
 Find sexual and reproductive health care in New Brunswick.
 Virtual Care NB
 Virtual Care NB can provide virtual consultations for common illnesses, injuries, infections or general health concerns.
