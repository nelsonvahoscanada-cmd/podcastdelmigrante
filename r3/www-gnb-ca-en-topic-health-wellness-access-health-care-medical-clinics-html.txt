Medical (walk-in) clinics
 In-person health-care consultations for a common illness, injury, infection or general health concerns. 
 On this page
 In case of an emergency
 If you are having difficulty breathing, chest pain or other severe symptoms, call 911 immediately or go to your local emergency room. Other health concerns would be considered non-emergency.
 Overview
 If you already have a primary care provider (family doctor or nurse practitioner), contact them first for non-emergency care. 
 Medical clinics provide care for New Brunswick patients with non-emergency medical problems who do not have a primary care provider (family physician or nurse practitioner) or who are unable to obtain an appointment with their primary care provider within a reasonable time frame. 
 Clinics can provide non-emergency medical care. Most clinics take same-day appointments and require booking ahead before showing up for an appointment. 
 Find a medical clinic near you
 In-person walk-in clinics:  Medical clinic locations (New Brunswick Medical Society) 
 Treatments
 Most appointments are covered with a valid New Brunswick Medicare card. There may be fees depending on the service required (i.e. sick notes) and some services are not provided (chest pain, abdominal pain assessment). 
 Some services offered by medical clinics include: 
 flu symptoms such as vomiting, diarrhea, chills and fever 
 sore throat 
 earache 
 eye infection 
 bladder infection 
 rash (such as scabies, etc.) 
 testing for sexually transmitted infections 
 lower back pain from lifting or twisting 
 order tests such as blood work, X-rays, MRIs, CT scans, scopes (such as colonoscopies, etc.) 
 Use the symptom checker to find out how serious your symptoms are, if a healthcare visit is needed and where to get help.
