Virtual Care NB
 Virtual Care NB can provide virtual consultations for common illnesses, injuries, infections or general health concerns. 
 On this page
 New Brunswickers can access Virtual Care NB by :
 using the symptom checker tool or
 by calling Tele-Care 811 
 New Brunswickers who do not have a primary care provider can book an appointment for a prescription renewal, laboratory testing or follow up appointment from previous Virtual Care NB appointment book online or call Tele-Care 811 .
 Overview
 Virtual Care NB is now the province's new integrated access point for virtual health services. 
 You should contact your primary care provider (family doctor or nurse practitioner) first for your health-care needs. 
 If you do not have a primary care provider OR your provider is unavailable for issues that cannot wait, Virtual Care NB can offer clinical care for your health needs. 
 Virtual Care NB can assess and treat many common, non-urgent illnesses, injuries, infections and general health concerns. This helps ensure patients receive the right care, in the right place, at the right time, while maintaining access to virtual care services across the province. 
 How to access Virtual Care NB
 Virtual Care NB appointments are available: 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., seven days a week.
 To determine if virtual care can help, you need to:
 Use the symptom checker – if virtual care is the right care for you, this will allow you to book a Virtual Care NB appointment. This is a required step to access Virtual Care NB. 
 OR 
 Call Tele-Care 811 and speak to a registered nurse. They can: 
 assess your symptoms 
 determine the best care option 
 connect you to the appropriate services, including booking a Virtual Care NB appointment, if recommended 
 Scheduled appointments with Virtual Care NB can be booked on the same day or the next day. You can participate in the appointment using a telephone, cell phone, computer or tablet. Video, telephone and chat consultation options are available. 
 No account or specific mobile app is required to access Virtual Care NB. 
 Before your Virtual Care NB appointment, you must have:
 completed the symptom checker assessment or have been assessed by a Tele-Care nurse 
 have valid New Brunswick Medicare card 
 be physically located in New Brunswick at the time of the appointment 
 be 16 years of age or older (if you are less than 16 years of age, an adult must book your appointment and be present during the virtual consultation with the practitioner) 
 If a nurse practitioner reviews your appointment details and determines your health concern would be better addressed through another type of care, you will be notified and provided with information on the appropriate next steps. 
 Appointments for children and dependents (i.e. a guardian)
 You can complete the symptom checker assessment and book a Virtual Care NB appointment on behalf of a child or dependent. However, during the appointment, you and the individual for whom the consult is intended will both need to be present.  
 When booking an appointment for someone else, you will enter the patient’s information. 
 Accessing health care in New Brunswick 
 Find the right care for your situation.
 Services
 Virtual Care NB is a bilingual service for anyone with a valid New Brunswick Medicare card . We are unable to accommodate consultations without a valid Government of New Brunswick Medicare card. 
 Other virtual care services and providers do exist but are not covered by Medicare and are at the expense of the patient. 
 Virtual Care NB can assess and treat many common illnesses, injuries, infections or general health concerns, including: 
 flu-like symptoms such as fever, chills, body ache and fatigue 
 sore throat 
 eye infection 
 bladder infection 
 testing for sexually transmitted infections 
 lower back pain from lifting or twisting 
 other non-emergency health concerns 
 The Virtual Care NB provider may request tests to ensure your treatment is appropriate. They may order tests such as: 
 blood work 
 urine and stool tests
 If you are experiencing addiction or mental health concerns, contact: 
 the free provincial addiction and mental health helpline: 1-866-355-5550 
 Canada Suicide Prevention Service: call or text 988 or visit 988.ca 
 Kids Help Phone: 1-800-668-6868 
 New prescriptions
 The Virtual Care NB service can prescribe medications based on the diagnosis during your consultation or lab results. 
 During your consultation, if a prescription is required, the provider will send your prescription to the pharmacy of your choice. You will need to contact the pharmacy to ensure the prescription has been received and is ready for pick up. 
 Prescription renewals
 Virtual Care NB providers are able to provide prescription renewals for New Brunswickers without a primary care provider, or those who have a primary care provider but are unable to access them in a timely manner. To book an appointment for a prescription renewal, book online or call Tele-Care 811 . 
 Pharmacists are also able to renew many prescriptions and can help New Brunswickers with this need.
 Laboratory testing
 Virtual Care NB providers can order routine laboratory work for New Brunswickers without a primary care provider. To book an appointment for a lab referral, book online or call Tele-Care 811 ..
 Follow ups for laboratory testing ordered by Virtual Care NB
 Individuals who require a follow-up from a previous Virtual Care NB appointment can book online or call Tele-Care 811 .
 When in-person care or care by another provider may be needed
 The Virtual Care NB service cannot address: 
 chest pain 
 abdominal pain 
 sick notes after an illness or without treatment 
 medical or insurance forms 
 counselling for mental health and addictions 
 health concerns that require an in-person physical assessment, including possible fractures
 If the Virtual Care NB provider determines that you would be best supported through an in-person assessment, or that they cannot help you with your request, they will direct you to the appropriate health-care service.
 Privacy
 All Virtual Clinic NB practitioners are licensed in the Province of New Brunswick and are in good standing with their professional regulatory organizations. Your privacy is of the utmost importance. You can learn more about how your data is handled by reading the Department of Health Privacy Notice . 
 Virtual Care NB will store all patient information securely in Canada. 
 eVisitNB health records
 Health records from the previous virtual care provider can be accessed by logging into your Maple account .
