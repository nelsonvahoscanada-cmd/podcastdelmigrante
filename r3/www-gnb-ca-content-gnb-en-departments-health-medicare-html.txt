 Medicare
Did you know there is a faster and easier way to apply for your Medicare card? It’s user friendly and we can process your application in less time. Click the Online Medicare Application form to begin.
 If you have already applied for Medicare, DO NOT submit another application by paper or online.
 Please allow 10 to 12 weeks for the processing of your application.
 If you have not received correspondence after 14 weeks, please contact Service New Brunswick Teleservices at 1-888-762-8600.
 If you are visiting New Brunswick, you are encouraged to purchase private emergency medical insurance for travelers.
 Information
Please note that New Brunswick residents are required to notify Medicare of any changes to their personal information, their status in Canada, their address, etc. Failure to do so may result in termination of Medicare coverage. Medicare has the right to terminate coverage or adjust an expiry date at any time.
