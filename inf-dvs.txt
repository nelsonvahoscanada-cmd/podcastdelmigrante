 Driver and Vehicle Services
 Driver and vehicle services are available in-person at various offices across the NWT as well as online at [https://www.idmv.inf.gov.nt.ca/] gov.nt.ca/DriverServices .
 In-Person Services
 [https://www.idmv.inf.gov.nt.ca/Help/Driver-and-Vehicle-Offices] Visit your local driver and vehicle office 
 Online Services
 The following services are available online:
 [https://www.idmv.inf.gov.nt.ca/Drivers] Driver's licences, identifcation cards, driver's manuals and driver abstract 
 [https://www.idmv.inf.gov.nt.ca/Vehicles] Renew, replace or cancel a vehicle registration 
 [https://www.idmv.inf.gov.nt.ca/Commercial] Commercial drivers services, including, permits and registration
