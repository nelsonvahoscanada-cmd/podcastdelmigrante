 Labour Force Activity
 The labour market provides an indication of how many people are employed or unemployed among the working age population. This information may assist in policy and program planning as well as educational and career decisions.
 Information on labour market activity for the Northwest Territories overall, is available monthly and annually from Statistics Canada’s Monthly Labour Force Survey. Community level information is available every 2 to 3 years from the Census and from the NWT Community Survey.

 Labour Force Survey Highlights - October 2026 
 Statistics Canada estimates that in October 2026, out of 34,600 Northwest Territories residents aged 15 years and older, 22,900 were employed, a decrease of 800 persons from the previous month. Consequently, the employment rate declined by 2.3 percentage points to 66.2%, while the unemployment rate increased to 5.8% in October.
