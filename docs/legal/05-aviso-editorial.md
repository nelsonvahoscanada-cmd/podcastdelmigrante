### 2. Información especializada: no es asesoría profesional

- **Migración.** El contenido no es asesoría migratoria. Solo pueden cobrar
  por asesorar o representar en trámites migratorios los consultores
  regulados por el College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants
  (CICC), los abogados y otros profesionales autorizados por la ley. Verifica
  siempre en IRCC (canada.ca) y en las fuentes oficiales de cada provincia.
- **Finanzas, impuestos y seguros.** El contenido no es asesoría financiera,
  fiscal, de inversión ni de seguros personalizada. Los productos, las tasas y
  las reglas cambian y dependen de cada persona.
- **Salud.** El contenido no reemplaza la consulta con un profesional de la
  salud. Ante una emergencia, llama al 911.
- **Vivienda y asuntos legales.** El contenido no es asesoría legal. Para
  casos concretos, consulta a un abogado o a los servicios oficiales, como la
  Residential Tenancies Dispute Resolution Service en Alberta.

Las leyes y los programas cambian. Indicamos la fecha de publicación y, cuando
corresponde, la de actualización, pero el contenido puede quedar

### 1. Our purpose

El Podcast del Migrante informs and supports Canada's Latin American migrant
community through news, guides, stories and interviews. We publish general,
educational information.

### 2. Specialized information: not professional advice

- **Immigration.** Content is not immigration advice. Only consultants
  regulated by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC),
  lawyers and other professionals authorized by law may charge for
  immigration advice or representation. Always verify with IRCC (canada.ca)
  and each province's official sources.
- **Finance, tax and insurance.** Content is not personalized financial, tax,
  investment or insurance advice. Products, rates and rules change and depend
  on each person.
- **Health.** Content does not replace consulting a health professional. In an
  emergency, call 911.
- **Housing and legal matters.** Content is not legal advice. For specific
  cases, consult a lawyer or official services, such as Alberta's Residential
  Tenancies Dispute Resolution Service.

Laws and programs change. We show the publication date and, where applicable,
the update date, but content may become outdated. We are not responsible for
decisions made solely on the basis of it.

### 3. Sources and verification

- We prioritize official sources (governments, regulators) and link to them
  under "Fuentes consultadas" (sources consulted).
- We distinguish fact from opinion. Columns and contributor pieces express
  their authors' opinions, are identified as such, and include the author's
  field of expertise where relevant.
