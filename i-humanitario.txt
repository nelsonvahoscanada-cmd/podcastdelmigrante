 Contact the Immigration humanitarian support desk 
 The Immigration humanitarian support desk provides help and resources for people affected by natural disasters and international crises. 
 The support desk: 
 provides information on federal programs to assist with immigration and family reunification;
 provides information on settlement services and availability of humanitarian aid; and
 guides new arrivals looking for employment opportunities in the Yukon. 
 To contact the support desk, email [/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#126077746775777761526b67797d7c3c7173] [email protected] or phone 867-667-9500. Phone toll free in the Yukon 1-800-661-0408 extension 9500.
 Refugee status 
 The United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (the United Nations Refugee Agency) and the Government of Canada determine refugee status in Canada.
 Only the Government of Canada can handle refugee and asylum claims. Provincial and territorial governments do not manage these claims. [https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/refugees/canada-role.html] Learn about how Canada’s refugee system works. 
 Employment opportunities 
 Foreign workers can use many jobs sites to search for work in the Yukon. Some sites include but are not limited to: 
 [https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/home] Canada Job Bank ;
 [https://www.employmentyukon.ca/] Employment Central ; and
 [https://www.yuwin.ca/] Yuwin .

 Resources for Ukrainians
 Contact the [https://yukonukrainians.ca/] Ukrainian Canadian Association of Yukon (UCAY) to connect with Ukrainians residing in the Yukon.
 Government of Canada resources
 Visit the Government of Canada’s refugees and [https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/refugees.html] asylum website . 
 Contact Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada through their [https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/contact-ircc/client-support-centre.html] Client Support Centre if you have questions about your application for:
 a visa;
 a temporary permit;
 a refugee claim; or
 permanent residence.
 On their website, you can:
 check [https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html] processing times of your application; or
 check your [https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-status.html] application status . 
 In Canada, the rights of all workers are protected. [https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/protected-rights.html] Know your rights. 
 Contact

 Our office is open from 8:30 am to 4:30 pm Yukon Time from Monday through Friday. If you're contacting us outside of these hours, send an email and you'll automatically receive our list of resources.
