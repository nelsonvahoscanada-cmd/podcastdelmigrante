NUNAVUT ARCTIC COLLEGE HAS FIVE CAMPUSES AND COMMUNITY LEARNING CENTERS LOCATED IN ALL 25 COMMUNITIES OF NUNAVUT WHICH BRING OUR PROGRAMS HOME TO PEOPLE THROUGHOUT THE TERRITORY. THESE CENTRES ARE A DOORWAY TO A WIDER WORLD OF LEARNING OPPORTUNITIES. EACH COMMUNITY LEARNING CENTRE IS STAFFED BY AN ADULT EDUCATOR WHO CAN PROVIDE INFORMATION AND GUIDANCE TO ANYONE SEEKING FURTHER EDUCATION.
NUNATTA CAMPUS

Arctic College's Nunatta Campus supports the Nunavut communities of Arctic Bay, Cape Dorset, Clyde River, Grise Fiord, Hall Beach, Igloolik, Iqaluit, Kimmirut, Pangnirtung, Pond Inlet, Qikiqtarjuaq, Resolute and Sanikiluaq. 

For more information:

Phone: 867-979-7200

Toll-Free: 1-866-979-7200

E-Mail: nunatta@arcticcollege.ca

KIVALLIQ CAMPUS- VIRTUAL TOUR!

Kivalliq Campus serves the Kivalliq Region of Nunavut and supports the communities of Arviat, Baker Lake, Chesterfield Inlet, Coral Harbour, Rankin Inlet, Repulse Bay and Whale Cove. In Rankin Inlet, Kivalliq Campus is home to Kivalliq Hall and the Sanatuliqsarvik Nunavut Trades Training Centre. 

For more information:

Phone: 867-645-4170

E-Mail: kivalliq@arcticcollege.ca

KITIKMEOT CAMPUS- VIRTUAL TOUR!

Kitikmeot Campus of Nunavut Arctic College supports the Nunavut communities of Kugluktuk, Gjoa Haven, Kuugaruk, Taloyoak and Cambridge Bay.

For more information:

Phone: 867-983-4111

Toll-Free: 1-866-383-4533

E-Mail: kitikmeot@arcticcollege.ca.
