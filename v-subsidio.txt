 Apply for a rent subsidy 
 People who have a low- to moderate-income can apply to get help with their rent from the Canada-Yukon Housing Rental Benefit program.
 When we receive applications, we:
 process and review them to see if they’re eligible; and
 send a letter to everyone who applies with details about their application or eligibility.
 If the program becomes fully subscribed we:
 continue to process applications; and
 put eligible applicants on a waitlist.
 We contact eligible applicants when there’s space in the program.
 What you need to be eligible 
 To be eligible for the Canada-Yukon Housing Rental Benefit you must:
 be a Canadian citizen or have permanent resident or refugee status;
 be a Yukon resident for at least 3 months;
 be renting a house in the Yukon and not receiving any other housing benefit or subsidy;
 have an annual household income (combined income of all adults 19 or older who are living in the home) under the [https://yukon.ca/en/housing-and-property/social-housing-tenant-supports/find-household-income-limit-thresholds] Affordable Household Income Limits for your eligible unit size (based on the [https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p3Var.pl?Function=DEC&Id=100731] National Occupancy Standards );
 have less than $100,000 in assets;
 file an annual Canadian Income Tax Return; and
 have income from a qualifying source such as:
 employment;
 Employment Insurance;
 short-term Workers Compensation;
 short-term disability;
 alimony payments;
 Training Allowance;
 Old Age Security;
 Guaranteed Income Supplement; or
 pension income.
 How to apply
 Fill out the [https://yukon.ca/en/yhc-canada-yukon-housing-rent-benefit-application] application form .
 Attach any required documents:
 For anyone 19 years old and over: 1 piece of government issued photo identification 
 for anyone 18 years old and under:1 piece of government issued identification 
 For all members of the household over 19 years old: a current [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/e-services/e-services-individuals/a-proof-income-statement-option-print.html] Proof of Income Statement from the Canada Revenue Agency (to contact the CRA phone 1-866-426-1527)
 ​a copy of your current lease agreement
 Submit your completed application form to Yukon Housing Corporation.
 In person: Drop your application off at a local [https://yukon.ca/en/find-housing-office] Yukon Housing Corporation office 
 Email: [/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3f57504a4c5651585d5a515a59564b7f464a545051115c5e] [email protected] 
 Fax: 867-667-3664

 Whitehorse, Yukon Y1A 2C6
 We'll send you a letter to let you know we've received your application.
