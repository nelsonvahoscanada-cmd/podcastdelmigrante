 Nunavut Arctic College celebrates World Teachers’ Day and recognizes educators across Nunavut 
 October 5, 2026 
 Iqaluit, Nunavut (October 5, 2026) – This World Teachers’ Day, Nunavut Arctic College is proud to recognize the teachers across Nunavut who help shape strong communities, support student success, and keep language and culture thriving in classrooms and communities every day.
 We’re also celebrating the next generation of Nunavut educators through the Nunavut Teacher Education Program (NTEP)! NTEP is a made-in-Nunavut pathway that helps Inuit and Nunavummiut become certified teachers while staying connected to home, community, and culture.

 Nunavut Arctic College commemorates Orange Shirt Day and National Day for Truth and Reconciliation
 October 1, 2026 
 On September 29, 2026, Nunavut Arctic College commemorated the day by holding a country food feast for local Elders prepared by staff and student volunteers at the Nunatta Campus in Iqaluit. A reflection wall was also set up to encourage students to reflect and share their thoughts on truth and reconciliation.
 In Rankin Inlet, at the Kivalliq Campus staff and students congregated for a feast, and to learn from Elders, Martha Kanayok and Simeonie Ammaq, as they spoke about tuberculosis as part of the Inuit experience.

 Nunavut Arctic College at the AFN Education Forum 
 February 27, 2026 
 Nunavut Arctic College was pleased to participate in the Assembly of First Nations (AFN) Education Forum in Vancouver, supporting Nunavut Teacher Education Program (NTEP) students as they engaged with Indigenous education leaders from across Canada. 
 Throughout the forum, participants highlighted shared priorities for Indigenous education, including Indigenous language revitalization, culturally grounded curriculum, and self-determined education systems led by Indigenous communities.
