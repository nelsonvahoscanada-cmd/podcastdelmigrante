 Find free newcomer services near you
 We fund many qualified professionals who can help you successfully adapt to life in Canada. You can get our free settlement services if you’re eligible .
 Who’s eligible for settlement services Updated 
 Changes to eligibility period for newcomer services 
 Starting April 1, 2026, economic class permanent residents can only access newcomer services for a limited time. 
 Find out more about 
 [https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/settle-canada/newcomer-services/eligibility.html] the changes to the eligibility period 
 [https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/notices/changes-settlement-service-eligibility-economic-immigrants.html] why we made the changes 
 You’re eligible for our services if you’re:
 a permanent resident (PR), including a resettled refugee
 a successful permanent residence applicant ( [https://www.canada.ca/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#approved] approved in principle ) 
 You got a letter confirming the approval from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
 a protected person who received a positive decision from the Immigration and Refugee Board (IRB)
 a temporary resident (or their [https://www.canada.ca/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#dependant] dependant ) in certain employment programs, such as:
 Atlantic Immigration Program
 Rural and Northern Immigration Program
 a person approved under certain pathways, such as:
 Palestinians who left Gaza on or after September 1, 2023, and their families (until March 31, 2027)
 a participant of Francophone Minority Communities Student Pilot (including their families)

 Working in Canada 
 Help finding a job
 Get help with your job search, including information on training programs and the job market.

 Regulated jobs include doctors, lawyers, architects, teachers, nurses, engineers and certified professional accountants. 
 Language 
 Language assessment
 Get your language ability (English or French) assessed so you can be placed in a language training class.

 Learning English
 Job-related language training

 Informal language learning
 Find services to learn English or French in an informal setting. Examples: conversation circles, literacy groups and book clubs.
 Language training for French speakers in Francophone minority communities
 Get language training in English ( [https://www.canada.ca/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#language_instruction_for_newcomers_to_canada] LINC ), French ( [https://www.canada.ca/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#clic] CLIC ) or both. This service addresses the needs of Francophone newcomers in French-speaking communities outside Quebec.
