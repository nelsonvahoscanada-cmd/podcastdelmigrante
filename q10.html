2026 Referendum Results

Question 10: Should Alberta remain a province of Canada, or should the Government of Alberta commence the legal process required under the Canadian Constitution to hold a binding provincial referendum on whether or not Alberta should separate from Canada?
Option 1 - Alberta should remain a province in Canada.
Option 2 - The Government of Alberta should commence the legal process required under the Canadian Constitution to hold a binding provincial referendum on whether or not Alberta should separate from Canada.

Event:

On October 19, several voting places across the province used one set of ballot boxes for all voting areas, rather one set for each voting area to facilitate voting area reporting. Due to this, results for 14 locations where voting area votes could not be reliably separated from each other are reported at the voting place level, rather than the voting area level. This combined result is shown against the first of those voting areas and other voting areas in that location will show zero. Voter turnout for these voting areas should be read at the electoral division level.
